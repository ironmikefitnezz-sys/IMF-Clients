Readiness Assessment
You came in today fully charged with perfect sleep, high energy, and zero soreness, so you are in the best possible position to put in a strong core and cardio session.
Warm-Up
Light Treadmill Walk
Easy walk to raise your heart rate and warm up.
5 minutes, gets the blood moving and raises your body temperature before the main work begins.
Cat-Cow
On hands and knees, arch your back up, then let it sink down, slowly.
10 reps, loosens up the spine and prepares your core for the work ahead.
Bird Dog
On hands and knees, reach one arm and the opposite leg straight out, then switch.
8 reps each side, activates the core stabilizers you will be relying on throughout the session.
Main Block
CORE BLOCK
Plank, Forearms
Hold a straight body line on your forearms and toes.
3 sets of 30 seconds, rest 45 seconds between sets.
Reverse Crunch
On your back, curl your knees toward your chest by lifting your hips slightly.
3 sets of 12 reps, rest 45 seconds between sets.
Bicycle Crunch
On your back, bring opposite elbow and knee together in a pedaling motion.
3 sets of 15 reps each side, rest 45 seconds between sets.
Pallof Press
Stand sideways to a cable, press the handle straight out and resist being twisted.
3 sets of 10 reps each side, rest 45 seconds between sets.
Side Plank
Lie on your side, lift your hips so your body forms a straight line, hold.
3 sets of 20 seconds each side, rest 45 seconds between sets.
Cable Woodchop, High to Low
Pull a cable from above one shoulder down across your body.
3 sets of 10 reps each side, rest 45 seconds between sets.
CARDIO BLOCK
Stair Climber, Steady
Step continuously on a machine with moving stairs.
12 minutes at a comfortable steady pace, no rest needed, focus on keeping your breathing controlled throughout.
Cool-Down
Lying Knee-to-Chest Stretch
On your back, hug one knee toward your chest.
30 seconds each side, releases tension in the lower back and hips after core work.
Lying Spinal Twist, Gentle
On your back, drop both knees to one side, shoulders stay down.
30 seconds each side, helps decompress the spine after all the core engagement.
Child's Pose
Kneel, sit back on your heels, reach your arms forward on the floor.
45 seconds, lets your core and back fully relax before you finish.
Diaphragmatic Breathing, 90/90 Position
On your back, calves on a bench, take slow deep belly breaths.
2 minutes, brings your heart rate down and reinforces the breathing patterns that carry over into your wrestling training.
Coach Note
A strong, stable core is one of the biggest advantages you can build before wrestling season, and today you laid down a solid baseline to build from.
