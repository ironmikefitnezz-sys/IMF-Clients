Readiness Assessment
Your numbers look solid today Rod, good sleep, strong energy, low stress, and that leg soreness is just your muscles responding to the work you put in two days ago, so you are in a great spot to train.
Warm-Up
5 to 7 minutes to get the chest primed and upper body blood flowing before you load anything. Arm circles, forward and backward, 30 seconds each direction. This loosens up the shoulder joint so it is not under stress when you start pressing. Band pull-aparts or light cable face pulls, 2 sets of 15 reps. This activates the upper back muscles that stabilize your shoulder blades during pressing, which protects your shoulders and makes your chest work harder. Light dumbbell chest press on a flat bench, 2 sets of 12 reps with a weight that feels easy. This is not a working set, it is a rehearsal for the movement pattern.
Main Block
Today is Tuesday, which means chest. Your legs are sore from Saturday's full body session, so nothing lower body touches today. All chest, all upper body, and you have the readiness to push with real intent. Flat Barbell Bench Press 4 sets. First set 12 reps as a build-up set with moderate weight. Sets 2 and 3, drop to 8 reps and increase the weight to something challenging but controlled. Set 4, aim for 6 reps at your heaviest weight of the day. Rest 90 seconds between sets. This is your primary mass builder for the session, treat it that way. Incline Dumbbell Press 3 sets of 10 reps. Set the bench to about a 30 to 45 degree angle. This shifts more work to the upper portion of the chest. Choose a weight where the last 2 reps of each set are genuinely hard. Rest 75 seconds between sets. Chest Dips (forward lean dip, leaning your chest slightly forward to keep the focus on your chest rather than your triceps) 3 sets of 10 to 12 reps using your bodyweight. If this feels too easy, use a dip belt to add weight. If the full range of motion is uncomfortable at the shoulder, stop at parallel. Rest 75 seconds between sets. Cable Chest Fly (standing cable fly, arms slightly bent, squeezing the chest as you bring your hands together in front of you) 3 sets of 12 to 15 reps. Use a weight that lets you fully feel the stretch at the start of each rep and a strong squeeze at the finish. This is not a strength movement, it is a detail movement, so control every rep. Rest 60 seconds between sets. Machine Chest Press or Hammer Strength Chest Press 2 sets of 12 to 15 reps. This is your finishing movement. Pick a moderate weight and focus on the full range of motion and a slow 2-second squeeze at the peak of each rep. Rest 60 seconds between sets.
Cool-Down
5 minutes to bring your body down and keep your chest and shoulders feeling good. Doorway or cable chest stretch, hold for 30 seconds each side. Step one arm against a wall or cable column and gently rotate away until you feel a stretch across the chest. This counteracts the shortening effect of all the pressing you just did. Shoulder cross-body stretch, pull one arm across your chest and hold 20 to 30 seconds each side. Keeps the rear shoulder loose after heavy pressing sessions. Child's pose or kneeling lat stretch, hold 30 to 45 seconds. Decompress the upper back and open up the lats which get recruited during heavy pressing work. Deep diaphragmatic breathing, 5 slow deep breaths. Inhale for 4 counts, hold for 2, exhale for 6. Brings your nervous system down after a high-output session.
Coach Note
Your leg soreness today is exactly why the fixed schedule works in your favor, Tuesday belongs to chest and that gives your legs the full recovery time they need while you keep building, so make those pressing sets count and stay focused on load progression from last week.
