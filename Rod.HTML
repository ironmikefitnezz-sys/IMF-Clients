Readiness Assessment
Rod, you are coming in today well-rested, energized, and with zero soreness, which means you are set up for a strong chest day.
Warm-Up
5 minutes to get the upper body ready to work. Start with 2 minutes of arm circles, alternating forward and backward, to get blood moving into the shoulders and chest. Move into 1 minute of band pull-aparts or a light cable face pull variation to open the chest and activate the rear shoulder. Finish with 2 minutes of light dumbbell chest press at 50 percent of your working weight, slow and controlled, to prime the pressing muscles and practice your range of motion before loading up.
Main Block
Exercise 1: Barbell Bench Press 4 sets of 8 reps. Choose a weight that feels challenging by rep 6. Rest 75 seconds between sets. Exercise 2: Incline Dumbbell Press 3 sets of 10 reps. Focus on a full stretch at the bottom and a strong squeeze at the top. Rest 60 seconds between sets. Exercise 3: Cable Chest Fly (using a cable machine set at chest height, arms slightly bent, bring handles together in front of you) 3 sets of 12 reps. Slow and controlled on the way out, squeeze hard on the way in. Rest 60 seconds between sets. Exercise 4: Dips (chest-focused version, lean your torso slightly forward as you lower yourself) 3 sets of 10 reps. Go down until your upper arms are roughly parallel to the floor. Rest 60 seconds between sets. Exercise 5: Push-Up to Failure (standard push-up, bodyweight only) 2 sets, go until form breaks down. Rest 45 seconds between sets.
Cool-Down
5 minutes to bring the chest and shoulders down. Hold a doorway or cable column chest stretch for 30 seconds on each side, letting the pec fully open up. Move into a cross-body shoulder stretch, pulling one arm across your chest and holding 30 seconds each side. Finish with 1 to 2 minutes of slow deep breathing, hands on your knees or lying flat, letting your heart rate settle and your chest muscles release.
Coach Note
You hit today with great numbers across the board so push the barbell bench with intention this session, because that compound movement is where you are going to drive the most muscle growth in your chest over time.
