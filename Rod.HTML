Readiness Assessment
Your numbers are solid today, Rod, and that mild leg soreness from Saturday is completely normal after a full body day, so we'll train smart around it and put everything into chest.
Warm-Up
5 to 7 minutes to get the chest, shoulders, and elbows ready to push. Arm circles, both forward and backward, 30 seconds each direction. This loosens up the shoulder joints before any pressing. Band pull-aparts or light dumbbell lateral raises, 2 sets of 15 reps. Gets the rear shoulder and upper back warmed up so your front shoulder stays stable under load. Incline push-ups, 2 sets of 10 reps at a slow controlled pace. Wakes up the chest and gets blood moving through the pressing muscles before you load the bar. Elbow circles and wrist rotations, 30 seconds. Prepares the joints for the grip and pressing demand ahead.
Main Block
Exercise 1: Flat Barbell Bench Press 4 sets of 8 reps, rest 90 seconds between sets. This is your primary chest builder for the day. Focus on lowering the bar under control to your chest and pressing through the full range. Add weight from your last chest session if the final reps felt manageable. Exercise 2: Incline Dumbbell Press 3 sets of 10 reps, rest 75 seconds between sets. Angle targets the upper chest, which responds well to volume at this rep range. Keep your elbows at about a 45 degree angle rather than flaring straight out. Exercise 3: Cable Chest Fly (cable crossover bringing the handles together in front of your chest) 3 sets of 12 reps, rest 60 seconds between sets. Keep a slight bend in the elbows throughout and focus on squeezing the chest at the center. This is about feeling the muscle work, not moving heavy weight. Exercise 4: Dumbbell Decline Press 3 sets of 10 reps, rest 75 seconds between sets. Hits the lower portion of the chest that flat pressing underloads. Control the descent and press with intention. Exercise 5: Push-Up Finisher (standard or weighted if available) 2 sets to 1 rep short of failure, rest 60 seconds between sets. End the session by flushing the chest with full bodyweight pressing. This builds muscular endurance in the chest and keeps total volume high without adding joint stress.
Cool-Down
5 to 7 minutes to bring the body down and protect the shoulder joints after heavy pressing work. Doorway or cable chest stretch, hold 30 seconds each side. Opens the chest and counteracts the forward position your body was in during all pressing movements. Cross-body shoulder stretch, hold 30 seconds each arm. Releases the rear shoulder which works hard as a stabilizer during bench pressing. Tricep overhead stretch, hold 30 seconds each arm. The triceps take significant load during pressing and benefit from a dedicated stretch. Deep breathing, 10 slow breaths with a focus on expanding the chest fully on each inhale. Brings your heart rate down and reinforces chest expansion after a session that compresses the thorax under load.
Coach Note
The leg soreness is your body finishing its job from Saturday, so redirecting everything into chest today is exactly the right call and keeps your muscle building momentum moving without losing a single training day.
