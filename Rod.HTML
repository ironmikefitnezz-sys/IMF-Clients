Readiness Assessment
Your numbers look solid today Rod, and a little leftover soreness in your legs from Saturday is completely normal after a full body session, so today's chest day actually lines up perfectly.
Warm-Up
5 to 7 minutes to get your chest, shoulders, and elbows ready to move well. Band pull-aparts or arm circles: 2 sets of 15 reps, moving slowly to open up your chest and shoulders. Incline pushups on a bench: 2 sets of 10 reps, controlled pace up and down, getting blood into the chest and warming up your elbows. Shoulder rolls and cross-body arm swings: 30 seconds each side, loosening the shoulder joint before any pressing begins.
Main Block
Today is Chest. All metrics are strong and your legs are the only area reporting soreness, so you are cleared for a full session with no restrictions on the upper body. Flat Barbell Bench Press: 4 sets of 8 reps. Lower the bar to your chest with control, press up strong. Rest 75 seconds between sets. This is your primary strength builder for the session. Incline Dumbbell Press: 3 sets of 10 reps. Set the bench to about a 30 to 45 degree angle. Lower the dumbbells slowly, press up and slightly inward at the top. Rest 60 seconds between sets. This targets the upper portion of your chest to build full thickness. Chest Dips (on parallel bars, leaning slightly forward): 3 sets of 10 reps. Keep a slight forward lean to keep the emphasis on your chest rather than your triceps. If bodyweight feels easy, add a weight belt or hold a dumbbell between your legs. Rest 60 seconds between sets. Cable Fly (standing at a cable machine, handles set at chest height): 3 sets of 12 reps. Arms slightly bent, bring the handles together in front of your chest like you are hugging a tree, squeeze at the center, then slowly open back out. Rest 45 seconds between sets. This is your finishing isolation movement to fully fatigue the chest muscle fibers. Push-Up Burnout (flat on the floor, standard grip): 2 sets to failure, meaning keep going until you cannot complete another clean rep. Rest 45 seconds between sets. This flushes the muscle with blood and rounds out total chest volume for the session.
Cool-Down
5 to 7 minutes to bring your heart rate down and reduce tightness in the chest and front of the shoulders. Doorway or cable machine chest stretch: Hold for 30 seconds each side, feeling the stretch across the front of your chest. Cross-body shoulder stretch: Pull one arm across your chest and hold 20 to 30 seconds each side. Child's pose or seated forward fold: 60 seconds to decompress your spine and bring your breathing back to baseline.
Coach Note
You are building real chest thickness right now Rod, and the combination of compound pressing and that cable fly finish is exactly the kind of stimulus that adds size without sacrificing the lean look you are working to keep, so stay tight on your rest periods and make sure every rep on the bench has a controlled lowering phase.
