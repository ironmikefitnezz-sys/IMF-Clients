Readiness Assessment
Harold, you came in today with great sleep, solid energy, and no soreness at all, so you are set up for a strong upper body session.
Warm-Up
8 to 10 minutes total given your age. Start with 2 minutes of light cardio such as a brisk walk or easy bike to get blood moving. Then move into arm circles forward and backward for 30 seconds each direction. Follow with shoulder rolls and cross-body arm swings for 1 minute to open up the shoulder joint. Add in band pull-aparts or a light resistance tube row for 2 sets of 15 to warm up the upper back and prepare the shoulder for pressing and pulling. Finish with neck circles and chest opener stretches holding each side for 20 seconds. This extended warm-up protects your right shoulder before any loading begins.
Main Block
Today is Monday, Upper Body. All exercises are selected to work around your right shoulder while still building strength and supporting your goal of dropping to 200 to 205 pounds. Exercise 1: Chest Press Machine Use the machine press instead of free weights to reduce rotator cuff demand on your right shoulder. 3 sets of 12 reps, rest 75 seconds between sets. Exercise 2: Seated Cable Row Sit at the cable row station, grip the handle, and pull it toward your lower chest keeping your elbows close to your sides. 3 sets of 12 reps, rest 75 seconds between sets. Exercise 3: Neutral Grip Dumbbell Press (palms facing each other) Lying on a flat bench, hold dumbbells with palms facing each other throughout the movement. This grip reduces stress on the right shoulder compared to a standard grip. 3 sets of 10 reps, rest 75 seconds between sets. Exercise 4: Lat Pulldown Sit at the pulldown machine, grip the bar just outside shoulder width, and pull the bar down to your upper chest. 3 sets of 12 reps, rest 75 seconds between sets. Exercise 5: Dumbbell Lateral Raise (performed seated) Sit on a bench, hold light dumbbells at your sides, and raise them out to shoulder height. Keeping this seated reduces any momentum that could strain the right shoulder. 3 sets of 12 reps, rest 75 seconds between sets. Exercise 6: Cable Bicep Curl Stand at the cable stack, attach a straight or EZ bar, and curl from hip height to shoulder height with controlled movement. 3 sets of 12 reps, rest 60 seconds between sets. Exercise 7: Cable Tricep Pushdown Using a rope or straight bar attachment, push the cable down until arms are fully extended, squeezing at the bottom. 3 sets of 12 reps, rest 60 seconds between sets.
Cool-Down
5 to 8 minutes. Start with a cross-body shoulder stretch holding each arm for 20 to 30 seconds to release tension from the right shoulder. Move into a doorway or cable machine chest stretch, placing your forearm on the frame and gently rotating your body away for 20 seconds each side. Follow with an overhead tricep stretch holding each arm for 20 seconds. Finish with slow deep breathing for 1 to 2 minutes, inhaling through the nose for 4 counts and exhaling through the mouth for 4 counts to bring your heart rate down gradually.
