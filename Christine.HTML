Readiness Assessment
You came in today with everything dialed in, Justin, great sleep, high energy, no soreness, and almost zero stress, so we are going to make the most of it.
Warm-Up
5 minutes to get the body ready for leg work. March in place with high knees for 60 seconds to get blood moving. Bodyweight glute bridges for 10 reps, slow and controlled, to wake up the hips and lower back. Standing hip circles, 10 each direction each leg, to loosen up the hip joints. Banded or unweighted lateral walks, 10 steps each direction, to activate the outer hips and glutes. Bodyweight box squat, 10 reps slow, to rehearse the movement pattern before loading.
Main Block
Today is Leg Day and your readiness is as good as it gets, so we are running full superset pairs. Superset 1 - complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Goblet Squat - hold a dumbbell at your chest, feet shoulder-width apart, squat down keeping your chest tall and knees tracking over your toes. 4 sets of 12 reps. Exercise B: Leg Curl Machine (lying or seated) - curl your heels toward your body with control, pause at the top, lower slowly. 4 sets of 12 reps. Rest 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 2 - complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Leg Press Machine - feet shoulder-width apart on the platform, press through your heels, do not lock your knees at the top, control the return. 4 sets of 10 reps. Exercise B: Hip Abduction Machine (outer thigh machine) - sit tall, press your knees outward against the pads with control, return slowly. 4 sets of 15 reps. Rest 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 3 - complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Leg Extension Machine - sit with your back flat against the pad, extend your legs out fully, lower with control. 3 sets of 15 reps. Exercise B: Hip Adduction Machine (inner thigh machine) - sit tall, squeeze your knees inward against the pads with control, return slowly. 3 sets of 15 reps. Rest 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Finisher - traditional set Glute Bridge with Dumbbell - lie on your back, dumbbell resting across your hips, feet flat on the floor, drive your hips up until your body forms a straight line from knees to shoulders, squeeze at the top, lower with control. 3 sets of 15 reps, rest 45 seconds between sets.
Cool-Down
5 to 7 minutes to bring the body down and protect your SI joint. Figure Four Stretch (lying piriformis stretch) - lie on your back, cross one ankle over the opposite knee, gently pull the uncrossed leg toward your chest until you feel a stretch in the crossed leg's hip. Hold 30 seconds each side. Kneeling Hip Flexor Stretch - from a half-kneeling position, gently shift your hips forward until you feel a stretch at the front of the back leg's hip. Keep your torso tall. Hold 30 seconds each side. Seated Hamstring Stretch - sit at the edge of a bench, extend one leg out straight, hinge forward slightly from the hips keeping your back flat until you feel a pull behind the thigh. Hold 30 seconds each side. Child's Pose - kneel on the floor, sit back toward your heels and reach your arms forward, let your lower back decompress and breathe slowly. Hold 45 to 60 seconds.
Coach Note
Justin, your right hip flexibility is a rehabilitation priority alongside your core, so never rush through those hip stretches in the cool-down, that work directly supports your SI joint recovery and keeps your leg training progressing safely toward that 10-pound goal.
