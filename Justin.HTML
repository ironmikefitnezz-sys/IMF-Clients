Readiness Assessment
You came in today firing on all cylinders, Justin, great sleep, high energy, and zero soreness means you are set up for a strong leg day.
Warm-Up
5 to 7 minutes to get your hips, knees, and lower back ready to move well. Band walks side to side: 2 sets of 10 steps each direction. Gets the glutes and hips activated before loading. Bodyweight glute bridge: 2 sets of 12 reps. Wakes up the glutes and gets your hips moving through their full range. Goblet squat with no weight or light dumbbell: 2 sets of 10 reps, slow and controlled. Primes the legs and reinforces the movement pattern you will use today. Leg swings front to back and side to side: 10 each leg. Opens up the hips and gets blood moving through the hip joint.
Main Block
Superset 1 - complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Goblet Squat - Hold one dumbbell at your chest and squat down like you are sitting back into a chair, keeping your chest tall. 4 sets of 10 reps. Exercise B: Leg Curl Machine (lying or seated) - Curl your heels toward your body against the resistance. 4 sets of 12 reps. Rest: 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 2 - complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Leg Press Machine - Feet shoulder width apart, press the platform away without locking your knees out at the top. 4 sets of 10 reps. Exercise B: Hip Abduction Machine - Sit in the machine and push your knees outward against the pads. 4 sets of 15 reps. Rest: 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 3 - complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Leg Extension Machine - Straighten your legs against the resistance, pause at the top for one second. 3 sets of 12 reps. Exercise B: Glute Bridge with Dumbbell on Hips - Lie on your back, place a dumbbell on your lower abdomen for added weight, drive your hips straight up and squeeze at the top. 3 sets of 15 reps. Rest: 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat.
Cool-Down
5 to 7 minutes to bring the body down and keep your hips and SI joint happy. Seated figure four stretch: Hold 30 to 40 seconds each side. Opens the outer hip and takes pressure off the SI joint. Lying single knee to chest pull: Hold 30 seconds each leg. Gently stretches the hip flexor and lower back. Kneeling hip flexor stretch: Hold 30 to 40 seconds each side. Releases tightness in the front of the hip built up during the session. Child's pose: Hold 45 to 60 seconds. Decompresses the lower back and lets everything settle.
Coach Note
Today was the kind of readiness that lets you actually push the leg work, and stacking these superset pairs gives you real volume without grinding your joints down, which keeps you moving toward that definition goal without setting your SI joint back.
