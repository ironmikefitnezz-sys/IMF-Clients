Readiness Assessment
You came in today with great sleep, high energy, and zero soreness, so you are set up perfectly to put in solid work toward that definition goal.
Warm-Up
5 to 7 minutes to get your shoulders and upper body ready to move well. Arm circles forward and backward, 10 each direction, to loosen up the shoulder joints. Band pull-aparts or a light resistance band held in front of you pulled out to the sides, 15 reps, to activate the muscles around the back of your shoulders. Shoulder pass-throughs with a resistance band or broomstick, 10 slow reps, to open up the chest and front of the shoulders. Light lateral raises with no weight or very light dumbbells, 15 reps, just to warm the movement pattern before you load it. Alt front raise with light dumbbells, 10 reps each side, easy pace.
Main Block
Superset 1 — complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Seated Dumbbell Shoulder Press — 4 sets of 10 reps. Sit upright, press the dumbbells directly overhead until your arms are almost fully extended, then lower back to ear level with control. Exercise B: Cable Face Pull — 4 sets of 15 reps. Set the cable at upper chest height, pull the rope toward your forehead with your elbows flared out and high, squeezing the back of your shoulders at the end of each rep. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 2 — complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Dumbbell Lateral Raise — 4 sets of 12 reps. Stand tall, lift the dumbbells out to your sides until they reach shoulder height, then lower slowly with control. The slow lowering is where the definition work happens. Exercise B: Dumbbell Front Raise — 4 sets of 10 reps. Alternating arms, lift one dumbbell straight in front of you to shoulder height, lower it, then repeat on the other side. Keep a slight bend in your elbow throughout. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 3 — complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Cable Upright Row — 3 sets of 12 reps. Stand in front of a low cable with a straight bar or rope attachment, pull the handle up toward your chin with your elbows leading and flaring out to the sides, then lower with control. Exercise B: Dumbbell Shrug — 3 sets of 15 reps. Hold dumbbells at your sides, shrug your shoulders straight up toward your ears, hold for one second at the top, then lower slowly. No rolling the shoulders. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat.
Cool-Down
5 minutes to bring everything down and keep your shoulders healthy long term. Cross-body shoulder stretch, 30 seconds each side. Pull one arm across your chest and hold it there, feeling the stretch in the back of that shoulder. Overhead tricep stretch, 20 seconds each side. Reach one arm overhead, bend at the elbow so your hand falls behind your head, and gently press down on that elbow with your opposite hand. Chest opener stretch, 30 seconds. Clasp your hands behind your back, gently lift your arms and open your chest toward the ceiling. Slow deep breathing, 5 breaths. Inhale for 4 counts, exhale for 4 counts. Let your heart rate settle fully before you head out.
Coach Note
This was a well-executed shoulder session and the combination of pressing and isolation work you did today is exactly what builds the shoulder definition you are after, so keep showing up like this and those results will follow.
