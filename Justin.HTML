Readiness Assessment
You came in today with great sleep, solid energy, and zero soreness, which means your body is fully ready to work and we are going to take full advantage of that.
Warm-Up
5 to 7 minutes to get your back, shoulders, and arms ready to move. Band pull-aparts or arm circles: 2 sets of 15 reps. Pull your arms out wide and squeeze your shoulder blades together at the end of each rep. This wakes up the muscles between your shoulder blades and gets your upper back ready to pull. Cat-cow stretch: 10 slow reps moving between arching and rounding your back. This gently mobilizes your spine and loosens up your lower back before any pulling work. Light dumbbell row: 1 set of 12 reps each side using a very light weight. This is not a working set, just a rehearsal to groove the pulling pattern and warm up your elbows before tricep work begins.
Main Block
Superset 1 - complete all 4 sets before moving on Exercise A: Seated Cable Row - sit at the cable row machine, grip the handle with both hands, pull it into your midsection, squeeze your shoulder blades together, then slowly let it return. 4 sets of 10 reps. Exercise B: Overhead Tricep Rope Extension - stand at the cable machine with the rope attachment set high, grip both ends, press your hands down and slightly outward until your arms are fully extended, then slowly return. 4 sets of 12 reps. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 2 - complete all 4 sets before moving on Exercise A: Single Arm Dumbbell Row - place one knee and hand on a bench for support, hold a dumbbell in the opposite hand, pull it straight up toward your hip, hold for a beat at the top, then lower with control. 4 sets of 10 reps each side. Exercise B: Tricep Dip Machine - sit in the dip machine, grip the handles, press down until your arms are fully extended, then slowly return to the start. 4 sets of 12 reps. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 3 - complete all 3 sets before moving on Exercise A: Straight Arm Cable Pulldown - stand at the cable machine with a straight bar or rope set high, keep your arms nearly straight, pull the bar down toward your thighs by driving your elbows toward the floor, then slowly return. 3 sets of 12 reps. Exercise B: Cable Tricep Pushdown with Straight Bar - stand at the cable machine, grip the bar with both hands, press straight down until your arms are fully extended, hold for a beat, then slowly return. 3 sets of 12 reps. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Finisher - traditional set, no superset Face Pulls - attach a rope to a cable machine at about face height, grip both ends with your palms facing in, pull the rope toward your face while flaring your elbows out wide, hold for a beat, then slowly return. 3 sets of 15 reps, rest 45 seconds between sets. This targets the rear of your shoulders and the muscles between your shoulder blades, reinforcing posture and protecting your shoulder joints long term.
Cool-Down
5 to 7 minutes to bring your heart rate down and release tension in the muscles you just worked. Child's pose with arm reach: Hold for 30 to 45 seconds. Sit back toward your heels, reach your arms forward on the floor, and let your lower back and lats relax completely. Doorway or cable machine chest and shoulder stretch: Stand facing a cable column or doorway, place your forearm against it, and gently rotate your body away until you feel a stretch across your chest and front shoulder. Hold 30 seconds each side. Overhead tricep stretch: Reach one arm straight up, bend it at the elbow so your hand drops behind your head, and use your other hand to gently press the elbow back. Hold 30 seconds each side. Slow deep breathing: Take 5 slow breaths in through your nose and out through your mouth. This helps your nervous system shift out of workout mode and speeds up recovery.
Coach Note
This is a strong baseline session for Back and Triceps, Justin, and since your body showed up completely fresh today, you now have a clear benchmark to build from as you work toward that definition goal over the coming weeks. This session uses two cable stations for the supersets, so if the gym is busy, have a dumbbell alternative ready for the cable row or the pushdown so you are not waiting between exercises.
