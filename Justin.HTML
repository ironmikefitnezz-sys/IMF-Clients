Readiness Assessment

Warm-Up
5 to 7 minutes to get your shoulders, upper back, and neck ready to move well. Band Pull-Aparts: 2 sets of 15 reps. Hold a light resistance band in front of you at chest height and pull it apart until your arms are fully extended to each side. This opens up the chest and activates the rear shoulder muscles before loading them. Arm Circles: 30 seconds forward, 30 seconds backward. Keep them slow and controlled, gradually increasing the size of the circle. This lubricates the shoulder joint and improves range of motion. Shoulder Rolls: 10 slow rolls forward, 10 backward. Shrug your shoulders up toward your ears, roll them back and down, then forward and down. Releases tension in the upper traps and neck. Face Pulls with Light Band: 2 sets of 15 reps. Anchor a band at face height, grab both ends, and pull toward your forehead while flaring your elbows out to the sides. Activates the rear shoulder and primes the rotator cuff for pressing and raising movements.
Main Block
Today is Shoulders. Your readiness is excellent so you are getting the full superset format. You will need two stations for each superset pair, so plan to grab your dumbbells and position yourself near a cable machine or bench as noted. Complete all sets of each superset before moving to the next pair. Superset 1: Complete all 4 sets before moving on. Exercise A: Dumbbell Shoulder Press (seated or standing, press the dumbbells from ear height straight up overhead and lower them back with control) -- 4 sets of 10 reps Exercise B: Dumbbell Lateral Raise (stand with dumbbells at your sides and raise them out to shoulder height like wings, then lower slowly) -- 4 sets of 12 reps Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat Superset 2: Complete all 4 sets before moving on. Exercise A: Cable Front Raise (attach a straight bar or rope to a low cable pulley, stand facing away from the machine, and raise the handle straight out in front of you to shoulder height, then lower with control) -- 4 sets of 12 reps Exercise B: Cable Rear Delt Fly (set the cable at shoulder height, grab the handle with the opposite hand, and pull it across your body and out to the side with a slight bend in the elbow, squeezing the back of your shoulder at the end) -- 4 sets of 12 reps each side Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat Superset 3: Complete all 3 sets before moving on. Exercise A: Dumbbell Arnold Press (start with dumbbells at chin height with palms facing you, rotate your palms outward as you press up overhead, then reverse the rotation on the way down) -- 3 sets of 10 reps Exercise B: Dumbbell Shrug (hold dumbbells at your sides, shrug your shoulders straight up toward your ears, hold for one second at the top, then lower slowly) -- 3 sets of 15 reps Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat
Cool-Down
5 to 7 minutes to bring your shoulders down and restore range of motion. Cross-Body Shoulder Stretch: Pull one arm across your chest with the opposite hand and hold for 30 seconds each side. Targets the rear shoulder and releases tension built up from pressing and raising work. Overhead Tricep and Lat Stretch: Raise one arm overhead, bend the elbow, and reach down your back. Use the opposite hand to gently press the elbow further back. Hold 30 seconds each side. Stretches the shoulder overhead position and the side of the upper back. Chest Opener Stretch: Clasp your hands behind your back, squeeze your shoulder blades together, and lift your arms slightly while opening your chest forward. Hold 30 seconds. Counteracts the forward shoulder position from pressing movements. Neck Side Stretch: Sit or stand tall, drop one ear toward your shoulder, and hold gently for 20 seconds each side. Releases the upper trap and neck tension common after shoulder training. Deep Breathing: 5 slow breaths in through the nose for 4 counts, hold for 2, out through the mouth for 6. Brings your heart rate down and signals your nervous system to shift into recovery mode.
