Today's Session  |  Active recovery. Sleep and energy are both below threshold for any external loading today.
Readiness Assessment
Your sleep and energy are both running low this morning, so today is about moving well and setting yourself up to train hard again soon.
Warm-Up
Light Bike
Easy pedaling with low resistance.
5 minutes, easy pace to gently raise your body temperature without taxing your system.
Arm Circles
Arms out to the sides, draw small then bigger circles.
30 seconds each direction, loosen up the shoulders before mobility work.
Wall Slides
Back against a wall, slide your arms up and down like making a snow angel.
10 reps, open up the upper back and shoulders with a controlled range of motion.
Main Block
Today is a full active recovery session. No external loading. Move through each exercise with intention and focus on breathing throughout.
Cat-Cow
On hands and knees, arch your back up, then let it sink down, slowly.
10 slow reps, move with your breath and let the spine decompress in both directions.
Bird Dog
On hands and knees, reach one arm and the opposite leg straight out, then switch.
3 sets of 8 reps each side, rest 30 seconds between sets. Keep your hips level and reach long through the opposite arm and leg.
90/90 Hip Switches
Sit with both knees bent at right angles, rotate your knees to the other side.
10 reps each side, rest 30 seconds between sets. Move slowly and let the hips open up without forcing range.
Standing Hip Circles
Stand on one leg, draw big slow circles with the other knee.
10 circles each direction, use this to work through any tightness in the hips from yesterday's leg session.
Clamshell with Band
Lie on your side, knees bent, band above knees, open the top knee like a clam.
2 sets of 15 reps each side, rest 30 seconds between sets. Controlled movement supporting right hip strength as part of your SI joint rehab.
Single-Leg Glute Bridge
Lift your hips with one foot on the floor and the other leg straight.
2 sets of 12 reps each side, rest 30 seconds between sets. Focus on keeping the pelvis level and squeezing at the top.
Plank, Forearms
Hold a straight body line on your forearms and toes.
2 sets of 20 seconds, rest 30 seconds between sets. Breathe steadily and hold without holding your breath.
Pallof Press
Stand sideways to a cable, press the handle straight out and resist being twisted.
2 sets of 10 reps each side, rest 30 seconds between sets. Slow press out and controlled return, this one directly supports your SI joint stability.
Foam Roll, Upper Back
Roller under your upper back, arms crossed, slowly roll.
60 seconds, work slowly up and down the upper back and pause on any tight spots.
Foam Roll, Glutes
Sit on a roller or ball, slowly roll over your glute muscles.
60 seconds each side, take your time here after two leg days this week.
Cool-Down
Figure-Four Stretch
Lie on your back, ankle on opposite knee, pull the legs toward you to stretch your glute.
45 seconds each side, target the right hip specifically to support your flexibility work.
Lying Knee-to-Chest Stretch
On your back, hug one knee toward your chest.
45 seconds each side, gentle decompression for the lower back and SI joint area.
Lying Spinal Twist, Gentle
On your back, drop both knees to one side, shoulders stay down.
30 seconds each side, let gravity do the work and breathe into the stretch.
Cross-Body Shoulder Stretch
Pull one arm across your chest with the other arm.
30 seconds each side, carry-over from the shoulder work earlier this week.
Diaphragmatic Breathing, 90/90 Position
On your back, calves on a bench, take slow deep belly breaths.
2 minutes, finish with slow deep breaths to calm the nervous system and close out the session.
Coach Note
Today is not a lost day, moving through this session keeps your consistency streak alive and the hip and core work here is directly building the foundation that will let you train harder and look leaner over time.
