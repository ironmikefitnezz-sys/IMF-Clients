Readiness Assessment
You came in today with great sleep, solid energy, and zero soreness, so you are set up for a strong Back and Triceps session that can push the work and start building a real baseline.
Warm-Up
5 to 7 minutes to get your back and shoulders moving and your elbows warmed up before any pulling or pushing. Band Pull-Aparts: 2 sets of 15 reps, holding a light resistance band at chest height and pulling it apart until your hands reach your sides. This opens up your chest and activates the muscles between your shoulder blades. Cat-Cow on the floor: 10 slow reps moving between arching and rounding your back. This wakes up your spine gently and supports your SI joint before loading. Arm Circles: 10 forward and 10 backward on each arm, keeping them controlled and full range. Prepares your shoulder joints for rows and pulldowns. Tricep Rope Pushdown with light weight: 15 reps at a very easy weight just to get blood moving into the elbow joint before working sets.
Main Block
Superset 1 — complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Seated Cable Row (sit at the cable row machine, pull the handle to your lower chest keeping your back upright, and squeeze your shoulder blades together at the end) — 4 sets of 10 reps Exercise B: Tricep Rope Pushdown (stand at the cable machine with the rope attachment, push the rope down and slightly apart at the bottom until your arms are straight, then control it back up) — 4 sets of 12 reps Rest: 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat Superset 2 — complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Lat Pulldown (sit at the pulldown machine, grip the bar slightly wider than shoulder width, pull it down to your upper chest while keeping your chest tall and leaning back only slightly) — 4 sets of 10 reps Exercise B: Overhead Tricep Extension with Dumbbell (hold one dumbbell with both hands, raise it overhead, then bend your elbows to lower the weight behind your head and press back up) — 4 sets of 12 reps Rest: 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat Superset 3 — complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Single Arm Dumbbell Row (place one knee and same-side hand on a bench for support, row the dumbbell up toward your hip keeping your back flat and parallel to the floor) — 3 sets of 10 reps each side Exercise B: Tricep Kickback with Dumbbell (hinge forward slightly at the hips, upper arm pinned to your side, extend the dumbbell straight back until your arm is fully straight, then return slowly) — 3 sets of 12 reps each side Rest: 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat Finisher — traditional set Straight Arm Pulldown (stand at the cable machine with a straight bar or rope, arms nearly straight, pull the bar down from shoulder height to your thighs using only your back muscles, not your biceps) — 3 sets of 15 reps, rest 45 seconds between sets
Cool-Down
5 to 7 minutes to bring your heart rate down and keep your back and elbows feeling good after the session. Child's Pose: Hold for 60 seconds, arms stretched forward, letting your lower back and the muscles along your spine fully relax. Great for your SI joint after a pulling session. Lat Stretch at the cable machine or squat rack: Hold a fixed point at shoulder height, step back and let your body drop slightly to feel a stretch along the side of your back. Hold 30 seconds each side. Overhead Tricep Stretch: Raise one arm overhead, bend the elbow so your hand reaches behind your head, and use your other hand to gently press the elbow back. Hold 30 seconds each side. Deep Breathing: 5 slow breaths in through the nose for 4 counts, out through the mouth for 6 counts. Helps your nervous system shift out of work mode.
Coach Note
This is your first Back and Triceps session on record so today is about establishing what you can do, picking weights that challenge you by the last two reps of each set without breaking your form, and logging those numbers so we have something real to build on toward that definition goal.
