Readiness Assessment
You came in today with great sleep, solid energy, and zero soreness, which means your body is fully ready to work and we are going to take full advantage of that.
Warm-Up
5 to 7 minutes to get your back, shoulders, and arms ready to move. Band pull-aparts or light cable face pulls: 2 sets of 15 reps. Stand tall, pull the band or cable apart at chest height, squeeze your shoulder blades together at the end of each rep. This wakes up the muscles between your shoulder blades and gets your upper back ready to pull. Tricep rope pushdowns with very light weight: 2 sets of 15 reps. Keep your elbows tucked at your sides and focus on feeling the back of your arm engage. This is just priming the movement, not working yet. Arm circles and shoulder rolls: 30 seconds forward, 30 seconds backward. Loosens up the shoulder joint before any pulling or pressing begins.
Main Block
Today is Back and Triceps. All metrics are strong so you are getting the full superset format. You will need two stations for each superset pair, so take a moment before each one to set up both pieces of equipment. Superset 1 — complete all 4 sets before moving on Exercise A: Seated Cable Row -- 4 sets of 10 reps. Sit at the cable row machine, grip the handle, keep your chest up and your back straight, and pull the handle toward your lower chest. Squeeze your shoulder blades together at the end of each rep before returning slowly. Exercise B: Tricep Rope Pushdown -- 4 sets of 12 reps. Stand at the cable machine with the rope attachment, keep your elbows pinned to your sides, and push the rope down until your arms are fully extended. Split the rope apart slightly at the bottom and squeeze. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 2 -- complete all 4 sets before moving on Exercise A: Lat Pulldown (wide grip, pulling the bar down to your upper chest) -- 4 sets of 10 reps. Sit at the pulldown machine, grip the bar just outside shoulder width, lean back slightly, and pull the bar down toward your collarbone. Control the return all the way up. Exercise B: Overhead Tricep Extension with Rope (cable set low, face away from machine, extend both arms overhead) -- 4 sets of 12 reps. Step away from the cable, lean slightly forward, and press both hands overhead until your arms are straight. Control the return. Keep your core tight throughout. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 3 -- complete all 3 sets before moving on Exercise A: Single Arm Dumbbell Row -- 3 sets of 10 reps each side. Place one hand and one knee on a bench for support. Hold a dumbbell in your free hand, let it hang, then pull it straight up toward your hip. Keep your back flat and your elbow close to your body. Exercise B: Dumbbell Skull Crusher (lying tricep extension, flat bench) -- 3 sets of 12 reps. Lie flat on a bench holding two dumbbells directly above your chest, palms facing each other. Bend only at the elbows and lower the dumbbells toward the sides of your head, then press back up. Keep your upper arms still throughout. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Finisher -- traditional set, no superset Straight Arm Pulldown (cable set high, arms mostly straight, pull bar down to your thighs) -- 3 sets of 15 reps. Stand back from the cable, hinge slightly at the hips, keep a soft bend in your elbows, and pull the bar all the way down to your thighs. This isolates the sides of your back without loading your lower spine.
Cool-Down
5 to 7 minutes to bring everything back down. Lat stretch in doorway or on cable machine: Hold a fixed point at shoulder height, step back, drop your hips slightly, and let your side stretch open. Hold 30 seconds each side. You will feel this along the sides of your back. Overhead tricep stretch: Raise one arm overhead, bend it at the elbow so your hand drops behind your head, and use your other hand to gently press the elbow back. Hold 30 seconds each side. Child's pose or seated forward lean: Sit on the floor or a bench, hinge forward, and let your back decompress. Hold for 60 seconds. This is especially good for your SI joint after any pulling session. Deep breathing: 5 slow breaths in through the nose, out through the mouth. Brings your heart rate down and helps your nervous system shift out of training mode.
Coach Note
Your back and triceps got a full, well-structured session today and that superset format is one of the most efficient ways to build the definition you are after, so keep showing up like this and the results will follow.
