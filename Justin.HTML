Readiness Assessment
You came in today feeling rested, energized, and completely fresh — that's about as good as it gets, and your body is ready to put in real work toward that definition and the 10 pounds you're after.
Warm-Up
5 to 7 minutes to get your shoulders, chest, and arms ready to move well. Arm circles: Stand tall and make slow, controlled circles with both arms — 10 forward, 10 backward. This gets blood moving into your shoulder joints before any pressing or pulling. Band pull-aparts or towel pull-aparts: Hold a resistance band or rolled towel in front of you at chest height with both hands shoulder-width apart. Pull it apart by squeezing your shoulder blades together, then return slowly. Do 15 reps. This opens up the chest and activates the upper back before your pressing work. Shoulder rotations with light band: Anchor a light band at chest height, hold it with one hand, and slowly rotate your arm outward and back. 10 reps each side. Warms up the shoulder joint and gets the rotator cuff firing. Light dumbbell chest press: Grab a light set of dumbbells and perform 15 slow, controlled reps lying on a flat bench. This is not a working set — just priming the movement pattern and getting your chest and biceps ready to load.
Main Block
Today is Chest and Biceps. All metrics are excellent so you are going into a full superset session. You will need two stations for each superset — plan your setup before you begin each pair so you are not scrambling between exercises. Superset 1 — complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Dumbbell Flat Bench Press — 4 sets of 10 reps. Lie on a flat bench, lower the dumbbells slowly to chest level, then press back up with control. Rest 45 to 60 seconds after completing both A and B, then repeat. Exercise B: Dumbbell Hammer Curl — 4 sets of 12 reps. Stand tall, palms facing each other, curl both dumbbells up toward your shoulders without swinging. Lower slowly. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 2 — complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Incline Dumbbell Press — 4 sets of 10 reps. Set the bench to about a 30 to 45 degree incline. Press the dumbbells up from chest level, focusing on the upper portion of the chest. Lower with control. Exercise B: EZ Bar Preacher Curl or Dumbbell Concentration Curl — 4 sets of 12 reps. Rest your upper arm on the preacher pad or your inner thigh if using a dumbbell, and curl the weight up slowly. Squeeze at the top, lower with full control. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 3 — complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Cable Chest Fly (cable crossover or pec deck machine) — 3 sets of 12 reps. Keep a slight bend in your elbows and bring your hands together in front of your chest, squeezing your pecs at the peak. Return slowly and with control. Exercise B: Cable Curl or Dumbbell Curl — 3 sets of 12 reps. Using a low cable pulley or a pair of dumbbells, curl with a full range of motion. Do not let your elbows drift forward at the top. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 4 — complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Push-Up (standard or decline for added upper chest emphasis) — 3 sets of 12 to 15 reps. Keep your core tight, lower your chest to just above the floor, and press back up without letting your hips sag. Exercise B: Dumbbell Alternating Curl — 3 sets of 10 reps per arm. Curl one arm at a time, keeping the opposite arm still at your side. Rotate your palm slightly upward as you curl to get a full contraction. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat.
Cool-Down
5 to 7 minutes to bring the session down and keep your chest and arms from tightening up. Doorway or cable chest stretch: Step into a doorway or use a cable at chest height. Place your hand on the frame or cable handle and gently rotate your body away until you feel a comfortable stretch across your chest. Hold 30 seconds each side. Overhead tricep and lat stretch: Reach one arm straight up, then bend at the elbow and let your hand fall behind your head. Use your other hand to gently press on that elbow. Hold 30 seconds each side. This also releases tension through the side of the body after pressing work. Bicep stretch against the wall: Extend one arm out to your side with your palm flat against a wall and your thumb pointing down. Gently rotate your body away from the wall until you feel the stretch along your bicep and forearm. Hold 30 seconds each side. Deep breathing cooldown: Stand or sit tall. Inhale through your nose for 4 counts, hold for 2, exhale through your mouth for 6. Repeat 5 times. Brings your heart rate down and helps your nervous system shift into recovery mode.
Coach Note
You are running two stations per superset today so scope out your setup before each pair — grab your dumbbells and position near the cable or bench so you are moving between A and B without losing time, and that efficiency is exactly what is going to keep your heart rate up and help chip away at that 10 pounds while you build the definition you are after.
