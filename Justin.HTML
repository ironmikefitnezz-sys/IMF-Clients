Readiness Assessment
You came in today firing on all cylinders, Justin, great sleep, high energy, zero soreness, and barely any stress on the radar, so we are going all in today.
Warm-Up
5 to 7 minutes to get your shoulders, neck, and upper back ready to move. Arm Circles: Stand with your arms out to your sides and make slow, controlled circles, gradually getting bigger. Do 10 forward, then 10 backward. This loosens up the shoulder joints before any loading. Band Pull-Aparts or Towel Pull-Aparts: Hold a resistance band or towel out in front of you at chest height with both hands about shoulder-width apart. Pull it apart by squeezing your shoulder blades together, then return slowly. Do 15 reps. This wakes up the muscles between your shoulder blades and stabilizes the joint. Shoulder Rolls: Roll your shoulders slowly forward 10 times, then backward 10 times. Helps release any tension built up overnight. Neck Half-Circles: Slowly drop your chin to your chest and roll your head gently from one shoulder to the other. Do 5 slow passes each direction. Keep it controlled, no full backward neck rolls. Wall Slides: Stand with your back against a wall, arms bent at 90 degrees and pressed lightly against the wall. Slowly slide your arms up overhead and back down, keeping contact with the wall the whole time. Do 10 reps. This primes your shoulder stabilizers for pressing and raising movements.
Main Block
Today is Shoulders. Yesterday was Legs, so your lower body did the work and your shoulders are completely fresh. All metrics are strong, so we are running full superset pairs today. A quick heads up: superset training means you will be moving between two pieces of equipment back to back. Before starting each superset, make sure both stations are set up and ready so you can transition smoothly without losing your rest window. Superset 1: Seated Dumbbell Shoulder Press paired with Dumbbell Lateral Raises Complete all 4 sets of this pairing before moving on. Exercise A: Seated Dumbbell Shoulder Press Sit upright on a bench with back support. Hold a dumbbell in each hand at shoulder height with palms facing forward. Press both dumbbells straight up overhead until your arms are almost fully extended, then lower them back to shoulder height in a controlled way. Do 4 sets of 10 reps. Exercise B: Dumbbell Lateral Raises Stand with a dumbbell in each hand at your sides, slight bend in your elbows. Raise both arms out to your sides until they reach shoulder height, then lower them back down slowly. Do not swing or use momentum. Do 4 sets of 12 reps. Rest 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 2: Dumbbell Front Raises paired with Rear Delt Fly (Bent-Over Dumbbell Fly) Complete all 3 sets of this pairing before moving on. Exercise A: Dumbbell Front Raises Stand holding a dumbbell in each hand in front of your thighs, palms facing you. Raise one arm straight out in front of you to shoulder height, lower it back down, then raise the other. Alternate arms for the full rep count. Do 3 sets of 12 reps per arm. Exercise B: Rear Delt Fly (Bent-Over Dumbbell Fly) Sit at the end of a bench and hinge forward so your chest is close to your thighs. Hold a dumbbell in each hand hanging below you with a slight bend in your elbows. Open your arms out wide to your sides, squeezing the muscles in the back of your shoulders, then lower slowly. Do 3 sets of 12 reps. Rest 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 3: Cable Face Pull paired with Dumbbell Arnold Press Complete all 3 sets of this pairing before moving on. Exercise A: Cable Face Pull Set a cable machine to about face height with a rope attachment. Stand facing the machine, grab the rope with both hands, and pull it toward your face by driving your elbows out wide and back. Your hands should end up beside your ears at the top. Return slowly. Do 3 sets of 15 reps. Exercise B: Dumbbell Arnold Press Sit upright holding a dumbbell in each hand at shoulder height with palms facing you, like the top of a curl. As you press the dumbbells overhead, rotate your palms forward so they face away from you at the top. Reverse the rotation as you lower them back to the start. Do 3 sets of 10 reps. Rest 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat.
Cool-Down
5 to 7 minutes to bring your shoulders and upper body back down. Cross-Body Shoulder Stretch: Bring one arm across your chest and hold it just above the elbow with your opposite hand. Hold for 20 to 30 seconds each side. Releases tension in the back of the shoulder. Overhead Tricep and Shoulder Stretch: Raise one arm overhead, bend it at the elbow so your hand drops behind your head, and gently press down on the elbow with your opposite hand. Hold 20 to 30 seconds each side. Chest Opener Stretch: Stand in a doorway or clasp your hands behind your back, open your chest, and lift your chin slightly. Hold 20 to 30 seconds. Counterbalances the pressing work from today. Neck Side Stretch: Tilt your ear toward your shoulder and hold gently for 15 to 20 seconds each side. Releases tension that builds up during overhead pressing. Deep Breathing Finish: Stand or sit tall, take 5 slow deep breaths in through your nose and out through your mouth. Brings your heart rate down and signals your nervous system to shift into recovery mode.
