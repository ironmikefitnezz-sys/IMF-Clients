Readiness Assessment
You came in today firing on all cylinders, Justin, great sleep, high energy, and zero soreness means you are ready to put in a strong Back and Triceps session.
Warm-Up
5 to 7 minutes to get your back, shoulders, and arms ready to work. Band Pull-Aparts: Stand holding a resistance band in front of you at chest height with arms straight. Pull the band apart by squeezing your shoulder blades together, then return slowly. Do 15 reps. This opens up the upper back and gets the muscles you are about to train firing properly. Cat-Cow: Get on all fours with hands under shoulders and knees under hips. Slowly arch your back up toward the ceiling, then let it drop down as you lift your head and chest. Move through this gently for 10 reps. This warms up your spine and loosens the lower back without putting any stress on your SI joint. Arm Circles: Stand tall and make slow, controlled circles with both arms, 10 forward and 10 backward. This gets the shoulder joints moving and increases blood flow to the arms and upper back. Light Lat Pulldown: Sit at the pulldown machine and use a very light weight. Pull the bar down to your chest and let it rise slowly. Do 15 easy reps. This is just to wake up the back muscles and rehearse the pulling pattern before you add real weight.
Main Block
Superset 1 - complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Seated Cable Row - Sit at the cable row machine with your feet on the platform and knees slightly bent. Pull the handle into your midsection, squeeze your shoulder blades together at the end, then let the cable pull your arms back out slowly and with control. 4 sets of 10 reps. Exercise B: Overhead Tricep Rope Extension - Stand at a cable machine with the rope attachment set at the top. Face away from the machine, hold the rope behind your head with elbows pointed forward, and press your hands forward and down until your arms are straight. Return slowly. 4 sets of 12 reps. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 2 - complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Lat Pulldown (Wide Grip) - Sit at the pulldown machine and grip the bar wider than shoulder width. Pull the bar down to your upper chest, squeeze the back at the bottom, then let it rise with control. 4 sets of 10 reps. Exercise B: Tricep Pushdown (Straight Bar or V-Bar) - Stand at a cable machine with the bar attachment set at the top. Grip the bar with palms facing down, keep your elbows pinned at your sides, and press the bar down until your arms are fully straight. Return slowly. 4 sets of 12 reps. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 3 - complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Single-Arm Dumbbell Row - Place one knee and the same-side hand on a flat bench for support. With the opposite hand, pick up a dumbbell and pull it up toward your hip, keeping your elbow close to your body. Lower it slowly. 3 sets of 10 reps each side. Exercise B: Dumbbell Overhead Tricep Extension - Sit on a bench with back support. Hold one dumbbell with both hands and raise it overhead. Lower it behind your head by bending your elbows, then press it back up. 3 sets of 12 reps. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 4 - complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Straight-Arm Cable Pulldown - Stand at a cable machine with a straight bar set at the top. Keep your arms nearly straight and pull the bar down toward your thighs by driving your elbows toward the floor. This isolates the back without bending the arms. 3 sets of 12 reps. Exercise B: Bench Dip - Sit on the edge of a flat bench with hands gripping the edge beside your hips. Slide forward off the bench and lower your body by bending your elbows to about 90 degrees, then press back up. Keep your back close to the bench throughout. 3 sets of 12 reps. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat.
Cool-Down
5 to 7 minutes to bring the body down and take care of your back and arms. Seat Stretch for the Back: Sit on a bench and lean forward slowly, letting your chest drop toward your knees and your arms hang between your legs. Hold for 20 to 30 seconds. You will feel a gentle stretch through the entire back. Lat Stretch at a Rack or Cable Machine: Stand and grab a fixed bar or the cable machine frame at about shoulder height with one hand. Lean your body away from that arm so you feel a stretch along the side of your back under your armpit. Hold 20 to 30 seconds each side. Tricep Stretch: Raise one arm overhead, bend the elbow so your hand drops behind your head, and use your other hand to gently press the elbow further back. Hold 20 to 30 seconds each side. Chest-Opening Stretch: Stand tall and clasp your hands behind your lower back. Gently squeeze your shoulder blades together and lift your chest slightly. Hold 20 to 30 seconds. This counterbalances all the pulling work you just did and keeps the shoulders balanced. Slow Deep Breathing: Take three to four slow deep breaths, inhaling for four counts and exhaling for six. This brings your heart rate down and signals recovery to your body.
Coach Note
Today's session pairs your back and triceps efficiently so each muscle group gets a real rest while the other works, and that structure is exactly what builds the kind of definition you are after, Justin, so focus on feeling the right muscles doing the work on every rep rather than just moving the weight. Note that Supersets 1 and 2 each require two cable stations at the same time, so scope out your setup before you start or swap one exercise for a dumbbell alternative if the gym is busy.
