Readiness Assessment
You came in today firing on all cylinders, Justin, great sleep, high energy, zero soreness, and low stress means you are set up for a strong chest and biceps session.
Warm-Up
5 to 7 minutes to get your chest, shoulders, and arms ready to work. Arm circles, forward and backward, 10 each direction. This loosens up the shoulder joint before any pressing. Band pull-aparts or a light resistance tube stretch across your chest, 15 reps. This activates the muscles between your shoulder blades and prepares your chest to move through a full range. Light dumbbell chest press on the bench, very light weight, 15 slow reps. This is rehearsal, not work. Focus on feeling your chest squeeze at the top. Alternating dumbbell curls, light weight, 12 reps each arm. Primes your biceps and elbow joints before loading them.
Main Block
Superset 1 - complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Dumbbell Bench Press - 4 sets of 10 reps. Lie flat on the bench, lower the dumbbells to chest level with control, press back up until your arms are almost fully extended. Feel your chest do the work. Exercise B: Dumbbell Hammer Curl - 4 sets of 12 reps. Stand tall, palms facing each other, curl both dumbbells up together or alternating. Keep your elbows locked at your sides the whole time. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 2 - complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Incline Dumbbell Press - 4 sets of 10 reps. Set the bench to about a 30 to 45 degree angle. Press the dumbbells up from chest level, focusing on the upper portion of your chest. Do not let the dumbbells flare out wide. Exercise B: Cable Curl (using the cable machine with a straight bar or EZ bar attachment at the low pulley) - 4 sets of 12 reps. Stand facing the machine, curl the bar up to shoulder height, squeeze at the top, lower slowly. The cable keeps tension on your biceps the whole way down. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 3 - complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Cable Chest Fly (using the cable machine with handles set at shoulder height, one on each side) - 3 sets of 12 reps. Stand in the center, step one foot forward for balance, bring both handles together in front of your chest like you are hugging a tree. Squeeze hard at the center, then open back out with control. Exercise B: Incline Dumbbell Curl - 3 sets of 10 reps. Set a bench to about a 45 degree angle, sit back against it with a dumbbell in each hand, let your arms hang straight down, then curl up. This position stretches the bicep muscle more than a standard curl and builds definition through a fuller range. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Finisher - traditional set, no superset pairing Machine Chest Press - 2 sets of 15 reps at moderate weight. This is your burnout to finish the chest work. Focus on controlled reps and feeling the squeeze, not moving heavy weight.
Cool-Down
5 to 7 minutes to bring your body down and keep your chest and arms from tightening up. Doorway chest stretch or cable column stretch, hold for 30 seconds each side. Step into a doorway or grab a cable column, let your chest open up fully. This is especially important after pressing work. Overhead tricep stretch, 20 seconds each arm. Reach one arm overhead, bend at the elbow, use your other hand to gently press the elbow back. Your triceps assisted all the pressing today and need attention. Cross-body shoulder stretch, 20 seconds each side. Pull one arm across your chest and hold. This targets the rear part of your shoulder that stabilizes every pressing movement. Slow deep breathing, 5 breaths in through the nose for 4 counts, out through the mouth for 6 counts. Drops your heart rate and starts the recovery process.
Coach Note
Justin, today was a perfect setup day and you took full advantage of it. The cable fly and incline curl in Superset 3 will directly target that chest and bicep definition you are after, so do not rush those reps. A heads up that Superset 3 requires the cable machine with both sides set up simultaneously, so grab your station before starting that block or swap one side for a dumbbell fly if the cable is occupied.
