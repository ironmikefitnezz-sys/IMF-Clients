Readiness Assessment
You came in today firing on all cylinders, Justin — great sleep, high energy, zero soreness, and basically no stress means you are primed for a strong leg day.
Warm-Up
5 to 7 minutes to get the hips, knees, and lower body ready to move well. Bodyweight Squats — 2 sets of 10 reps, slow and controlled, sitting back like you are lowering into a chair. This wakes up the legs and gets your hips moving through a full range. Glute Bridges — 2 sets of 12 reps, lying on your back with knees bent, pressing your hips straight up and squeezing at the top for one second. This activates the glutes and gently warms up the SI joint before loading. Banded Hip Abduction (standing, light band around ankles) — 2 sets of 10 reps each side, slow and deliberate. Opens up the hips and gets the stabilizers around your right hip engaged before the work begins. Leg Swings — 10 forward and back plus 10 side to side on each leg, holding a rack or wall for balance. Loosens the hip flexors and improves range of motion going into the session.
Main Block
Superset 1 — complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Goblet Squat — 4 sets of 10 reps, holding a dumbbell or kettlebell at your chest, sitting back and down with a tall spine, controlled on the way down and strong on the way up Exercise B: Leg Curl Machine (lying or seated) — 4 sets of 12 reps, curling your heels toward your body with control, squeezing at the top and lowering slowly Rest: 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat Superset 2 — complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Leg Press Machine — 4 sets of 10 reps, feet shoulder-width apart in the middle of the platform, pressing through the whole foot, stopping just before your knees fully lock at the top Exercise B: Hip Abduction Machine — 4 sets of 15 reps, pressing your knees outward against the pads with control, pausing briefly at the widest point before bringing them back in Rest: 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat Superset 3 — complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Leg Extension Machine — 3 sets of 12 reps, extending your legs out fully and squeezing the front of your thighs for one second at the top, lowering slowly Exercise B: Glute Bridge with Dumbbell (hip thrust on the floor) — 3 sets of 15 reps, lying on your back with a dumbbell resting on your hips, pressing up through your heels and squeezing your glutes hard at the top for one second Rest: 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat Finisher — traditional set, no superset Cable Pull-Through — 3 sets of 12 reps, facing away from a low cable machine, hinging at the hips and pushing them back while the cable pulls between your legs, then driving your hips forward to stand tall and squeeze your glutes at the top. Keep a flat back throughout.
Cool-Down
5 to 7 minutes focused on the hips, glutes, and lower back to support your SI joint recovery. Seated Piriformis Stretch (figure-four stretch in a chair) — hold 30 seconds each side, crossing one ankle over the opposite knee and gently leaning forward until you feel a stretch deep in the outer hip and glute. This directly targets the right hip flexibility that is a rehab priority for you. Kneeling Hip Flexor Stretch — hold 30 seconds each side, one knee on the ground and the other foot forward, gently pressing your hips forward until you feel a stretch at the front of the back leg. Keeps the hip flexors from tightening after all that hip work. Child's Pose — hold 60 seconds, sitting back toward your heels with arms stretched out in front, letting your lower back decompress gently after the session. Lying Knee-to-Chest Stretch — hold 30 seconds each side, pulling one knee gently into your chest while lying on your back. Releases tension around the SI joint and lower back.
Coach Note
Today was the kind of readiness that lets you actually push forward, so the load and volume today should be a step up from your last leg session — track what you lifted and aim to either add a rep or a small amount of weight next Thursday so you are building real momentum toward that muscle definition goal.
