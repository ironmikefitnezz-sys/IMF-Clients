Readiness Assessment
You came in today with great sleep, solid energy, and zero soreness, so we are going to make the most of it and put together a strong Back and Triceps session.
Warm-Up
5 to 7 minutes to get the upper body ready to work. Band pull-aparts: 2 sets of 15 reps. Hold a resistance band in front of you at chest height and pull it apart by squeezing your shoulder blades together. This wakes up the muscles between your shoulder blades before any pulling work. Cat-cow stretch: 10 slow reps. On all fours, round your back up toward the ceiling then drop your belly toward the floor in a controlled rhythm. This loosens up the spine and protects your SI joint before loading. Arm circles: 20 reps each direction, forward then backward. Keeps the shoulder joints mobile and warm before pressing or pulling. Light lat pulldown: 1 set of 15 reps at a very easy weight. Just to feel the back muscles engage before the real work begins.
Main Block
Superset 1 - complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Seated Cable Row - 4 sets of 10 reps. Sit at the cable row machine, keep your chest tall, pull the handle into your midsection, and squeeze your shoulder blades together at the end of each rep. Control the return slowly. Exercise B: Tricep Rope Pushdown - 4 sets of 12 reps. Stand at the cable machine with the rope attachment, keep your elbows pinned to your sides, and push the rope down until your arms are fully extended. Squeeze at the bottom. Rest: 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 2 - complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Lat Pulldown (wide grip) - 3 sets of 10 reps. Sit at the pulldown machine, grip the bar just outside shoulder width, and pull it down to your upper chest while leaning back slightly. Focus on driving your elbows down toward your hips. Exercise B: Overhead Tricep Extension with Rope - 3 sets of 12 reps. Face away from the cable stack, hold the rope overhead with both hands, and extend your arms straight up without flaring your elbows out. Lower slowly and feel the stretch at the bottom. Rest: 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 3 - complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Single-Arm Dumbbell Row - 3 sets of 10 reps each side. Place one knee and hand on a bench for support, keep your back flat, and row the dumbbell up toward your hip. Do not rotate your torso. This hits the back from a different angle than the cable work. Exercise B: Tricep Kickback with Dumbbell - 3 sets of 12 reps each side. Hinge forward slightly with a flat back, keep your upper arm parallel to the floor, and extend the dumbbell back until your arm is straight. Squeeze at the top. Rest: 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Finisher - traditional set Straight-Arm Pulldown - 2 sets of 15 reps. Stand at the cable machine with a straight bar or rope, arms slightly bent, and push the bar down in a wide arc from shoulder height to your hips. Keep your core braced throughout. This isolates the back without involving the biceps much, which is ideal at the end of a session.
Cool-Down
5 minutes to bring the body down and protect that SI joint. Seated forward fold: Hold for 30 seconds. Sit on the floor with legs extended, reach toward your feet, and let your lower back gently decompress. Child's pose: Hold for 45 seconds. Great for the lower back and hips after any session that loads the spine, even indirectly. Overhead tricep stretch: 20 seconds each arm. Reach one arm overhead, bend at the elbow, and use the other hand to gently press the elbow back. Doorway or cable chest and shoulder stretch: 30 seconds. Helps release the front of the shoulders after all the pulling work today. Deep breathing: 5 slow breaths in through the nose, out through the mouth. Brings your heart rate down and signals recovery.
Coach Note
Today's session is built around pulling from multiple angles and finishing the triceps thoroughly, which is exactly the kind of varied stimulus that builds the definition you are working toward. You will need two stations for each superset, so scope out the cable machine and a dumbbell rack before you start so you are not hunting for equipment mid-set.
