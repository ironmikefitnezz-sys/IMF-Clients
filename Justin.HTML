Readiness Assessment
You came in today with great sleep, solid energy, and zero soreness, so you are set up for a strong back and triceps session.
Warm-Up
5 to 7 minutes to get your back, shoulders, and arms ready to work. Band Pull-Aparts or Chest Openers: Stand tall and pull a light resistance band or your arms apart across your chest, squeezing your shoulder blades together. Do 2 sets of 15 slow and controlled reps. This opens up your upper back and gets the muscles that support your spine firing before you load them. Cat-Cow Stretch: Get on all fours and slowly round your back up toward the ceiling, then let it drop toward the floor. Move through 10 slow cycles. This wakes up your spine and helps your SI joint move freely before the session. Arm Circles and Tricep Swings: Stand with arms out to your sides and make slow circles, 10 forward and 10 backward. Then swing your arms across your chest and back out. 10 reps. Primes the shoulder joint and elbow for pulling and pressing movements.
Main Block
Today is Back and Triceps using superset pairs. You will need two stations for each superset, so scout your cable station and dumbbell area before you start. Each superset pairs a back movement with a tricep movement so one muscle rests while the other works. Superset 1 — complete all 4 sets before moving on Exercise A: Seated Cable Row — sit at the cable row machine, feet flat on the platform, grab the handle with both hands, pull it toward your belly button while squeezing your shoulder blades together, then slowly let it return. 4 sets of 10 reps. Exercise B: Tricep Rope Pushdown — stand at the cable machine with the rope attachment at the top, grip one end in each hand, push both ends down and slightly out until your arms are fully straight, then slowly return. 4 sets of 12 reps. Rest: 50 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 2 — complete all 3 sets before moving on Exercise A: Lat Pulldown (Wide Grip) — sit at the lat pulldown machine, grab the bar just outside shoulder width, pull it down to your upper chest while leaning back slightly, then slowly let it rise back up. 3 sets of 10 reps. Exercise B: Overhead Dumbbell Tricep Extension — sit on a bench, hold one dumbbell with both hands overhead, lower it behind your head by bending your elbows, then press it back up. 3 sets of 12 reps. Rest: 50 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 3 — complete all 3 sets before moving on Exercise A: Single Arm Dumbbell Row — place one hand and the same side knee on a bench for support, hold a dumbbell in the opposite hand, pull it up toward your hip while keeping your back flat, then lower it with control. 3 sets of 10 reps each side. Exercise B: Dumbbell Tricep Kickback — hinge forward slightly at the hips with a flat back, hold a dumbbell in each hand with elbows bent at your sides, straighten your arms back behind you until they are fully extended, then return slowly. 3 sets of 12 reps. Rest: 50 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Finisher — traditional set, no superset Straight Arm Cable Pulldown — stand at the cable machine with a straight bar or rope at the top, keep your arms nearly straight, pull the bar down toward your thighs by driving your elbows toward the floor, then slowly return. This targets the lower portion of your back without any spinal compression. 3 sets of 12 reps, rest 50 seconds between sets.
Cool-Down
5 to 7 minutes to bring your back and arms down from the work. Seated Forward Fold Stretch: Sit on the floor with legs straight out, reach both hands toward your feet and hold for 30 seconds. Releases tension through your entire back without putting pressure on your SI joint. Child's Pose: Kneel and sit back toward your heels, reach both arms forward on the floor, and let your back relax and lengthen. Hold for 45 seconds. One of the best gentle stretches for your lower back after a pulling session. Overhead Tricep Stretch: Raise one arm overhead, bend it so your hand drops behind your head, and use your other hand to gently press the elbow back. Hold 20 seconds each side. Releases the back of the arm after all that pressing work. Doorway or Cable Post Chest and Shoulder Opener: Grab a stable post or the cable machine frame with one hand at shoulder height and gently rotate your body away until you feel a stretch across your chest and front shoulder. Hold 20 seconds each side. Balances out the pulling work you just did.
Coach Note
This is your first back and triceps session on record, so today is about setting a strong, clean baseline that we can build on over time, which is exactly the kind of consistency that is going to get you to that 10 pound goal.
