Readiness Assessment
You came in today firing on all cylinders, Justin, great sleep, high energy, no soreness, and low stress means you are ready to put in real work toward that definition goal.
Warm-Up
5 minutes to get the upper body primed and ready to move. Arm circles, forward and backward, 30 seconds each direction. This loosens up the shoulder joints before any pressing or curling begins. Band pull-aparts or a light resistance band stretched across your chest pulled outward, 2 sets of 15 reps. This activates the muscles between your shoulder blades and gets the chest ready to work. Light dumbbell chest press on a flat bench, 1 set of 15 reps with very light weight. This is just warming up the movement pattern, not a working set. Alternating dumbbell curls, 1 set of 15 reps with very light weight. Same idea, just getting blood into the biceps before the real sets begin.
Main Block
Today is Chest and Biceps. All metrics are strong so you are getting the full superset format. You will need two stations for each pairing, so either set up dumbbells nearby or be ready to move quickly between pieces of equipment. Superset 1 - complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Flat Dumbbell Chest Press - 4 sets of 10 reps. Lie on a flat bench, press the dumbbells straight up over your chest, lower them with control until your elbows reach bench level, then press back up. Exercise B: Alternating Dumbbell Bicep Curl - 4 sets of 10 reps per arm. Stand tall, curl one dumbbell up to shoulder height while keeping your elbow pinned at your side, lower it with control, then switch arms. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 2 - complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Incline Dumbbell Chest Press - 3 sets of 12 reps. Set the bench to about a 30 to 45 degree incline, press the dumbbells up and slightly inward, lower with control. This targets the upper portion of the chest. Exercise B: Cable Bicep Curl (both arms, using a straight bar attached to the low cable pulley) - 3 sets of 12 reps. Stand facing the cable machine, curl the bar up to shoulder height keeping your elbows tucked in, lower slowly and with control. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 3 - complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Cable Chest Fly (using the cable machine set to chest height, one handle in each hand, step forward and bring your hands together in front of your chest) - 3 sets of 15 reps. Keep a slight bend in your elbows throughout and squeeze your chest at the midpoint. Exercise B: Hammer Curl (holding the dumbbells with your palms facing each other the entire time) - 3 sets of 15 reps. Curl both dumbbells up at the same time, keeping your elbows close to your sides, lower with control. This hits the outer bicep and forearm, which adds to overall arm definition. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 4 - complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Dumbbell Chest Fly (flat bench, arms wide, slight bend in elbows, bring the dumbbells together over your chest like you are hugging a tree) - 3 sets of 12 reps. Lower slowly and stop when you feel a stretch across your chest, then bring back up. Exercise B: Concentration Curl (seated, elbow braced against the inside of your thigh, curl one dumbbell at a time) - 3 sets of 12 reps per arm. Move slowly and squeeze at the top. This isolates the bicep peak directly. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat.
Cool-Down
5 to 7 minutes to bring the heart rate down and stretch out what you just worked. Chest doorway stretch, stand in a doorway, place both forearms on the frame at shoulder height, step one foot forward gently until you feel a stretch across the front of your chest. Hold 30 seconds, repeat twice. Overhead bicep stretch, extend one arm straight out in front of you, rotate your palm upward and facing away, gently press down on your fingers with your other hand until you feel a stretch along the inside of your forearm and bicep. Hold 20 seconds each arm. Cross-body shoulder stretch, bring one arm across your chest and hold it there with your other arm just above the elbow. Hold 20 seconds each side. This helps decompress the shoulder joint after pressing. Deep belly breathing, 5 slow breaths in through your nose for 4 counts, hold 2 counts, out through your mouth for 6 counts. This brings your nervous system down and supports recovery.
