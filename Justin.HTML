Readiness Assessment
You came in today firing on all cylinders, Justin, great sleep, high energy, zero soreness, and barely any stress on the radar, so we are going to put every bit of that to work.
Warm-Up
5 to 7 minutes to get your back and triceps ready to move well and load heavy. Band pull-aparts: 2 sets of 15 reps, holding a light resistance band in front of you and pulling it apart across your chest. This wakes up the muscles between your shoulder blades and gets your upper back primed. Cat-cow stretch on all fours: 10 slow reps, moving your spine gently up and down. This gets your lower back and core loose without putting any stress on your SI joint. Tricep pushdown with light weight or band: 2 sets of 15 reps, easy pace. Just warming up the elbow joint and getting blood into the back of your arms. Arm circles, 10 forward and 10 backward, then shoulder rolls for 30 seconds to open up the shoulder joint before pulling movements begin.
Main Block
Superset 1 - complete all 4 sets before moving on Exercise A: Seated Cable Row - sit at the cable row machine, grab the handle with both hands, pull it into your midsection while keeping your chest up and back straight, then slowly return. 4 sets of 10 reps. Exercise B: Cable Tricep Pushdown - stand at the cable machine with a rope or bar attachment, press the handle down until your arms are straight, then slowly let it return. 4 sets of 12 reps. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 2 - complete all 4 sets before moving on Exercise A: Single-Arm Dumbbell Row - place one knee and hand on a bench for support, row a dumbbell up toward your hip with the opposite arm, keeping your back flat. 4 sets of 10 reps per side. Exercise B: Overhead Tricep Extension with Dumbbell - hold one dumbbell with both hands overhead, lower it behind your head by bending your elbows, then press back up. 4 sets of 12 reps. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 3 - complete all 3 sets before moving on Exercise A: Lat Pulldown - sit at the pulldown machine, grip the bar slightly wider than shoulder-width, pull it down to your upper chest while leaning back slightly, then let it rise slowly. 3 sets of 10 reps. Exercise B: Tricep Dip Machine - sit in the dip machine and press the handles down until your arms are straight, then slowly return. 3 sets of 12 reps. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 4 - complete all 3 sets before moving on Exercise A: Face Pull with Cable - set the cable at upper chest height, grab the rope with both hands and pull it toward your face while flaring your elbows out to the sides. This targets the upper back and rear shoulders. 3 sets of 15 reps. Exercise B: Straight-Bar Tricep Pushdown - stand at the cable with a straight bar, press down until arms are fully extended, then slowly return. 3 sets of 15 reps. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat.
Cool-Down
5 to 7 minutes to bring your body down and keep your back feeling good. Child's pose: Hold for 45 seconds. Sit your hips back toward your heels and reach your arms forward on the floor. This gently stretches the entire back without loading the spine. Doorway or cable machine tricep stretch: Reach one arm overhead, bend the elbow, and use your other hand to gently press the elbow back. Hold 30 seconds each side. Single-arm cross-body stretch: Pull one arm across your chest and hold for 30 seconds each side. This releases tension in the upper back and rear shoulder. Slow deep breathing for 60 seconds, inhale for 4 counts, exhale for 6 counts. Helps your nervous system shift out of training mode.
Coach Note
Today was a strong session and every bit of that volume was earned by showing up recovered and ready. Keep stacking sessions like this and that definition goal you are after starts showing up in the mirror faster than you think. This session uses two cable stations in some supersets, so if the gym is busy, swap the pushdown to a dumbbell overhead extension or a bench dip to keep the pace moving without waiting.
