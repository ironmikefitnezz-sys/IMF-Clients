Readiness Assessment
You came in today firing on all cylinders, Justin, great sleep, zero soreness, and low stress means we have a perfect green light to build something solid for your shoulders.
Warm-Up
5 to 7 minutes to get your shoulders, rotator cuff, and upper back ready to move well. Arm circles, forward and backward: 20 reps each direction, slow and controlled, loosening up the shoulder joint before any loading. Band pull-aparts or towel pull-aparts: 2 sets of 15 reps, holding a resistance band or rolled towel in front of you and pulling it wide across your chest, activating the muscles around the back of your shoulder. Scapular wall slides: 10 slow reps, standing with your back flat against a wall, arms bent at 90 degrees, sliding them overhead and back down while keeping contact with the wall. This wakes up the muscles that stabilize your shoulder blades. Light dumbbell lateral raise, controlled tempo: 1 set of 12 reps with very light weight, focusing on smooth movement and full range before the working sets begin.
Main Block
Superset 1 - complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Seated Dumbbell Shoulder Press - 4 sets of 10 reps. Sit upright, press the dumbbells from ear height straight overhead without locking your elbows at the top, then lower with control. Exercise B: Dumbbell Lateral Raise - 4 sets of 12 reps. Stand with a slight bend in your elbows, raise the dumbbells out to your sides until they reach shoulder height, then lower slowly. Do not swing or use momentum. Rest: 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 2 - complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Dumbbell Front Raise - 3 sets of 12 reps. Stand tall, raise one or both dumbbells straight in front of you to shoulder height, lower with control. Alternate arms or go both together. Exercise B: Rear Delt Fly with Dumbbells (bent-over dumbbell reverse fly) - 3 sets of 12 reps. Hinge slightly at the hips with a flat back, raise the dumbbells out to your sides with a slight bend in the elbows, squeezing the muscles at the back of your shoulders at the top. Rest: 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 3 - complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Cable Upright Row - 3 sets of 12 reps. Stand at a cable machine with a straight bar or rope attachment set low, pull the handle up toward your chin keeping your elbows higher than your wrists, then lower with control. Exercise B: Face Pull with Rope Attachment - 3 sets of 15 reps. Set the cable at upper chest height, pull the rope toward your face with your elbows flared out wide, separating your hands at the end of the movement. This protects and strengthens the back of the shoulder joint. Rest: 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat.
Cool-Down
5 minutes to bring your shoulders down and maintain the flexibility work that supports your SI joint recovery. Cross-body shoulder stretch: Hold each arm across your chest for 30 seconds each side, feeling the stretch at the back of your shoulder. Doorway or cable column chest and shoulder opener: Stand in a doorway or next to a cable column, place your forearm on the frame and gently rotate your chest away until you feel a stretch across the front of your shoulder. Hold 30 seconds each side. Child's pose with arms extended forward: Kneel and reach both arms out in front of you on the floor, letting your chest drop toward the ground. Hold for 60 seconds. This gently decompresses your lower back and stretches your lats and shoulders together, which is a good finish for your SI joint as well. Slow neck rolls, side to side: 5 gentle reps each direction to release any tension carried in the neck and upper traps during pressing movements.
Coach Note
Today was a great day to lay down a strong baseline for your shoulders, Justin, and the variety of angles we hit today including front, side, and rear delt work sets you up to build the definition you are after as you get your consistency locked back in.
