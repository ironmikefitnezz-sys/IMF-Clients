Readiness Assessment
You came in today firing on all cylinders, Justin, great sleep, high energy, zero soreness, and barely any stress, so we are going to make every bit of that count.
Warm-Up
5 to 7 minutes to get your hips, knees, and ankles ready to work. Banded Side Steps: Place a light resistance band just above your knees and step side to side for 15 steps each direction. This wakes up the muscles around your hips and gets your SI joint moving safely before any loading begins. Bodyweight Glute Bridges: Lie on your back, feet flat on the floor, and drive your hips straight up by squeezing your glutes. Hold the top for 2 seconds, lower slowly. Do 12 reps. This activates your glutes and gently engages your core before the session. Leg Swings Front to Back: Stand next to a wall for balance and swing one leg forward and back in a controlled arc. 10 swings each leg. This opens up your hip flexors and improves range of motion through the hip joint. Bodyweight Box Squat: Sit back onto a bench or box slowly, pause briefly, then stand back up. Keep your chest up and your weight through your heels. 10 reps. This grooves the movement pattern before adding any weight.
Main Block
Superset 1 -- complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Goblet Squat -- Hold one dumbbell vertically at your chest, feet shoulder-width apart, sit your hips back and down, keeping your chest tall. 4 sets of 12 reps. Exercise B: Leg Curl Machine (lying or seated) -- Curl your heels toward your body against the resistance, squeeze at the top, lower slowly. 4 sets of 12 reps. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 2 -- complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Leg Press Machine -- Feet shoulder-width apart and mid-height on the platform. Press through your heels to full extension without locking your knees, lower slowly and controlled. 4 sets of 12 reps. Exercise B: Hip Abduction Machine (outer thigh machine) -- Sit tall, press your knees outward against the pads as far as comfortable, return slowly. 4 sets of 15 reps. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 3 -- complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Leg Extension Machine -- Sit with your back flat against the pad, extend your legs out to full extension, squeeze your quads at the top, lower slowly. 4 sets of 12 reps. Exercise B: Hip Adduction Machine (inner thigh machine) -- Sit tall, squeeze your knees inward against the pads, hold briefly at the end, return slowly. 4 sets of 15 reps. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Finisher -- traditional set, no superset Glute Bridge with Dumbbell (weighted glute bridge) -- Lie on your back with a dumbbell resting across your hips, feet flat and knees bent. Drive your hips up by squeezing your glutes hard, hold for 2 seconds at the top, lower with control. 3 sets of 15 reps, rest 60 seconds between sets. This directly supports your SI joint rehab by strengthening the glutes and stabilizing the pelvis.
Cool-Down
5 to 7 minutes to bring your body down and support your SI joint recovery. Seated Figure Four Stretch (piriformis stretch): Sit on a bench, cross one ankle over the opposite knee, and gently press down on the raised knee while sitting tall. Hold 30 seconds each side. This targets right hip flexibility, one of your rehab priorities. Kneeling Hip Flexor Stretch: From a half-kneeling position with one knee on the floor, shift your hips forward slightly until you feel a stretch through the front of the hip and thigh. Keep your core lightly braced. Hold 30 seconds each side. Child's Pose: From your knees, sit your hips back toward your heels and reach your arms forward along the floor. Breathe deeply and hold for 45 seconds. This gently decompresses the lower back and gives your SI joint a chance to settle after the session. Deep Belly Breathing: Sit or lie down, place one hand on your belly. Inhale slowly through your nose for 4 counts, let your belly rise, exhale for 6 counts. Repeat 6 to 8 times. This brings your nervous system down and supports full recovery.
