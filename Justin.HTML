Readiness Assessment
You came in today firing on all cylinders, Justin, great sleep, high energy, zero soreness, and barely any stress, so we are going to make the most of it.
Warm-Up
5 to 7 minutes to get your back, triceps, and joints ready to work. Band pull-aparts or light cable face pulls: 2 sets of 15 reps, slow and controlled, waking up the muscles between your shoulder blades and getting your upper back loose. Tricep cable pushdown with very light weight: 2 sets of 15 reps, just to get blood moving into the elbows and warm up the joints before loading them. Arm circles, both forward and backward, 10 reps each direction, loosening up the shoulders so they can support the pressing and pulling movements today. Cat-cow stretch on the floor or a bench: 10 slow reps moving your spine gently up and down, protecting your SI joint before the session begins.
Main Block
Superset 1 - complete all 4 sets before moving on Exercise A: Seated Cable Row - 4 sets of 10 reps. Sit tall, pull the handle into your lower chest, squeeze your shoulder blades together at the end of each rep, then slowly let the weight return. Rest 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Exercise B: Overhead Tricep Rope Extension - 4 sets of 12 reps. Use the rope attachment on a cable set at a low position. Grip the rope, press your arms straight overhead until fully extended, then slowly lower back down. Keep your elbows pointed forward and still throughout. Superset 2 - complete all 4 sets before moving on Exercise A: Lat Pulldown (wide grip cable pulldown to your chest) - 4 sets of 10 reps. Grip the bar just outside shoulder width, lean back slightly, and pull the bar down to your upper chest. Squeeze your back at the bottom, then slowly return to the top. Exercise B: Tricep Dip Machine - 4 sets of 12 reps. Sit in the machine, grip the handles, and press down until your arms are fully extended, then slowly return. If your gym does not have a dip machine, swap this for a bench dip with feet flat on the floor. Superset 3 - complete all 3 sets before moving on Exercise A: Single Arm Dumbbell Row - 3 sets of 10 reps each side. Place one knee and the same-side hand on a bench for support, keep your back flat and parallel to the floor, then row the dumbbell up toward your hip. Lower slowly. Exercise B: Cable Tricep Kickback - 3 sets of 12 reps each side. Use a single handle on a low cable. Hinge slightly at the hips, keep your upper arm pinned to your side, and extend your arm straight back until fully locked out. Slowly return. Finisher - traditional set, no superset Straight-Arm Cable Pulldown - 3 sets of 15 reps. Stand facing a high cable with a straight bar or rope. Keep your arms nearly straight with a soft bend at the elbow, and pull the bar down in a wide arc until it reaches your thighs. This targets the outer back and helps build the width you are working toward. Rest 60 seconds between sets.
Cool-Down
5 to 7 minutes to bring everything down and protect your SI joint after the session. Child's pose: Hold for 30 to 45 seconds, letting your lower back and hips decompress after all the rowing movements. Doorway or cable chest stretch: Stand in a doorway or use a cable at chest height, place your forearm on the frame, and gently lean forward to open up the chest and front of the shoulder. Hold 20 to 30 seconds each side. Overhead tricep stretch: Reach one arm straight up, bend the elbow so your hand drops behind your head, and gently press the elbow back with your other hand. Hold 20 to 30 seconds each side. Seated spinal rotation: Sit tall on a bench, cross your arms over your chest, and slowly rotate your torso left and right, 10 reps each direction, keeping the movement gentle and controlled.
Coach Note
Today is a great day to push the work and build real definition in your back and triceps, Justin, so focus on feeling the muscle work on every rep rather than just moving the weight, because that mind-muscle connection is what turns effort into results.
