Readiness Assessment

Warm-Up

Push with your legs, lean back slightly, pull the handle to your ribs, at a light effort.
4 minutes, gets the back and arms warmed up and blood moving before loading.
Wall Slides
Watch form video
Back against a wall, slide your arms up and down like making a snow angel.
10 reps, opens up the shoulders and upper back to prep for pulling movements.
Thread the Needle
Watch form video
On hands and knees, slide one arm under your body to twist your upper back.
6 reps each side, loosens up the mid-back rotation you will need for rowing.
Bird Dog

On hands and knees, reach one arm and the opposite leg straight out, then switch.
8 reps each side, activates the core and stabilizes the lower back before the main block.
