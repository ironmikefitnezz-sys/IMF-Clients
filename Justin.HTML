Readiness Assessment
You came in with a 9 on sleep, a 9 on energy, zero soreness, and almost no stress — that is about as dialed in as it gets, and today's session is going to reflect that.
Warm-Up
5 minutes total. Start with 2 minutes of light cardio — treadmill walk or bike — just enough to get blood moving. Then move into hip circles, standing on one leg at a time, 10 slow circles each direction per leg. Follow that with glute bridges on the floor, 10 slow reps, pausing at the top for 2 seconds each time. Finish with bodyweight box squats, 10 reps at a controlled pace, sitting back fully before standing. This sequence wakes up your hips and glutes and gets your SI joint ready to work without any compressive load.
Main Block
Today is Wednesday — Back and Triceps day. Superset pairs format. Hold two stations when you can, and see the Coach Note for a substitution tip if equipment is tied up. Superset 1 — complete all 4 sets before moving on Exercise A: Seated Cable Row — 4 sets of 12 reps. Sit tall, pull the handle into your lower chest, squeeze your shoulder blades together at the end of each rep, then return slowly. Control the return — do not let the cable yank you forward. Exercise B: Cable Tricep Pushdown with Straight Bar — 4 sets of 12 reps. Keep your elbows locked at your sides, press the bar down until your arms are fully straight, then return slowly to the top. Do not flare your elbows out. Rest: 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 2 — complete all 4 sets before moving on Exercise A: Lat Pulldown — 4 sets of 10 reps. Grip the bar slightly wider than shoulder width, lean back just slightly, and pull the bar down to your upper chest. Squeeze at the bottom, then let the bar rise slowly and under control. Exercise B: Overhead Tricep Extension with Rope (cable attachment) — 4 sets of 10 reps. Stand facing away from the cable stack, hold the rope overhead with both hands, elbows pointing forward, and extend your arms fully above your head. Lower slowly back behind your head. Rest: 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 3 — complete all 3 sets before moving on Exercise A: Single Arm Dumbbell Row — 3 sets of 12 reps per side. Place one knee and hand on a bench for support, keep your back flat and parallel to the floor, and row the dumbbell straight up toward your hip. Lower it slowly. Exercise B: Dumbbell Skull Crushers (lying tricep extension) — 3 sets of 12 reps. Lie flat on a bench, hold a dumbbell in each hand directly above your chest with palms facing each other, then bend at the elbows only to lower the weights toward the sides of your head. Press back up without locking out aggressively. Rest: 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat.
Cool-Down
5 to 7 minutes. Start with a doorway or cable column chest and shoulder stretch — hold 30 seconds per side. Move into a seated floor hamstring stretch, legs straight out, lean forward from the hips with a flat back, hold 30 seconds. Then do a lying glute stretch on the floor — pull one knee across your body gently and hold 30 seconds per side, which also gives your SI joint some gentle decompression. Finish with slow neck rolls and a 60-second deep breathing hold, exhaling fully on each breath out.
Coach Note
Justin, your numbers today are exactly what you want to see when you are working toward that 10-pound drop and starting to carve out some definition — high energy and zero soreness means you can train with real intent today, so push the rows and pulldowns hard on the back side and make the tricep movements slow and deliberate on the return, because that controlled lowering is where the definition work actually happens. If a cable station is taken when you need both for a superset, swap the tricep pushdown for a dumbbell kickback and keep moving.
