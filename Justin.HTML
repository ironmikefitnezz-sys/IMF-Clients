Readiness Assessment
Justin, you came in today firing on all cylinders with great sleep, high energy, zero soreness, and barely any stress on your plate.
Warm-Up
5 to 7 minutes to get the shoulders warmed up and moving well before loading them. Arm Circles: 30 seconds forward, 30 seconds backward. Start small and gradually get bigger to loosen up the shoulder joint. Band Pull-Aparts or Towel Pull-Aparts: 2 sets of 15 reps. Hold a resistance band or towel in front of you at chest height and pull it apart by squeezing your shoulder blades together. This activates the muscles around your shoulder blades before pressing and raising movements. Shoulder Rotations with Light Plate or Dumbbell: 2 sets of 10 slow circles in each direction. Hold a light weight in front of you and make slow controlled circles to warm up the rotator cuff. Dynamic Cross-Body Arm Swings: 20 reps total, alternating arms. Swing each arm across your chest and open it back out to the side to increase blood flow and range of motion through the shoulder.
Main Block
Superset 1 — complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Dumbbell Shoulder Press — 4 sets of 10 reps. Sit on an upright bench, press both dumbbells from shoulder height straight up overhead, then lower them back down with control. Keep your core tight throughout. Exercise B: Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift (straight-leg deadlift keeping your back flat) — 4 sets of 12 reps. Stand with dumbbells in front of your thighs, hinge at your hips while keeping a flat back, lower the weights along your legs until you feel a stretch in your hamstrings, then drive your hips forward to stand back up. This pairs with the shoulder press so your upper body rests while your lower posterior chain works, and it doubles as hip and glute work that supports your SI joint rehab. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 2 — complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Dumbbell Lateral Raise — 3 sets of 12 reps. Stand with a dumbbell in each hand at your sides, raise both arms out to the side until they reach shoulder height, then lower them back down slowly. Control the lowering portion. Exercise B: Cable Rope Face Pull — 3 sets of 15 reps. Set a cable machine at upper chest or eye level with a rope attachment. Grip both ends of the rope, step back to create tension, and pull the rope toward your face while flaring your elbows out to the sides. This targets the rear part of your shoulder and the muscles around your shoulder blades. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 3 — complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Dumbbell Front Raise — 3 sets of 12 reps. Stand holding dumbbells at your sides, raise one or both arms straight in front of you to shoulder height, then lower with control. Avoid swinging. Exercise B: Machine Rear Delt Fly (reverse pec deck) — 3 sets of 12 reps. Sit facing the pad of a pec deck machine, grip the handles with your arms slightly bent, and open your arms out to the sides by squeezing the back of your shoulders together. This targets the rear shoulder which is important for balanced shoulder development and posture. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Finisher — Traditional Set Dumbbell Shrug — 3 sets of 15 reps. Hold a dumbbell in each hand at your sides, shrug your shoulders straight up toward your ears, hold for one second at the top, then lower slowly. No rolling the shoulders.
Cool-Down
5 to 7 minutes to bring the shoulders down and maintain mobility. Cross-Body Shoulder Stretch: Hold each arm across your chest for 30 seconds per side. Pull the arm gently with your opposite hand to feel the stretch through the back of the shoulder. Overhead Tricep and Shoulder Stretch: Raise one arm overhead, bend the elbow so your hand reaches toward the opposite shoulder blade, and gently press the elbow back with your other hand. Hold 30 seconds per side. Chest Opener Stretch: Interlace your fingers behind your back, straighten your arms, and gently lift them while opening your chest toward the ceiling. Hold 30 to 45 seconds. This counteracts the forward shoulder position from pressing movements. Deep Belly Breathing: 5 slow breaths in through the nose and out through the mouth. Inhale for 4 counts, exhale for 6 counts. Brings your heart rate down and helps your nervous system shift out of training mode.
Coach Note
Your shoulder development is going to be a real driver of that defined, athletic look you are after, so hitting these consistently is where the work compounds over time. Superset 2 uses a cable station and a separate dumbbell space simultaneously, so scout both out before you start that pair or have a dumbbell alternative ready to go.
