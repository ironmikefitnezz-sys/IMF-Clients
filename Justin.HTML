Your Daily Coaching Session
Justin
2026-10-07
Today's Session  |  Moderate, traditional sets with full rest
Readiness Assessment
Sleep and energy are both running low today, so we are pulling back the effort a bit and letting quality movement do the work instead of chasing heavy loads.
Warm-Up
Arm Circles
Arms out to the sides, draw small then bigger circles.
30 seconds each direction, loosens up the shoulder joints before any pressing or raising.
Wall Slides
Back against a wall, slide your arms up and down like making a snow angel.
10 slow reps, gets the shoulder blades moving properly and primes the muscles around the joint.
Standing Hip Circles
Stand on one leg, draw big slow circles with the other knee.
10 reps each side, keeps the hips mobile after yesterday's leg session.
Light Bike
Easy pedaling with low resistance.
5 minutes at an easy pace, brings the heart rate up gently without taxing your energy reserves.
Main Block
Traditional sets today. Take the full rest between every set and focus on clean movement over heavy weight.
Machine Shoulder Press
Sit and push the handles overhead.
3 sets of 10 reps, rest 60 seconds between sets. The machine gives you a stable path so you can press with control even when energy is low.
Dumbbell Lateral Raise
Lift dumbbells out to your sides to shoulder height.
3 sets of 12 reps, rest 60 seconds between sets. Raise to shoulder height and resist on the way down.
Cable Front Raise
Lift a cable handle straight in front to shoulder height.
3 sets of 10 reps, rest 60 seconds between sets. Lift to shoulder height and lower with control.
Face Pull
Pull a rope to your face with elbows high, squeezing the upper back.
3 sets of 15 reps, rest 60 seconds between sets. Pull to forehead height and hold briefly at the end of each rep.
Band Pull-Apart
Hold a band in front and pull it apart.
2 sets of 15 reps, rest 45 seconds between sets. Keep a slight bend in the elbows and pull the band apart smoothly.
Cool-Down
Cross-Body Shoulder Stretch
Pull one arm across your chest with the other arm.
30 seconds each side, releases tension across the back of the shoulder.
Upper Trap Stretch
Tilt your ear toward your shoulder and gently add pressure with your hand.
30 seconds each side, eases tightness through the neck and top of the shoulder.
Lying Knee-to-Chest Stretch
On your back, hug one knee toward your chest.
30 seconds each side, gives the hips and lower back a gentle release after yesterday's leg work.
Diaphragmatic Breathing, 90/90 Position
On your back, calves on a bench, take slow deep belly breaths.
2 minutes, helps the nervous system settle and supports recovery when sleep has been short.
Coach Note
On low sleep days like today, pulling the intensity back a notch is exactly the right call because it keeps you consistent without digging a recovery hole that slows your progress toward that definition goal.
