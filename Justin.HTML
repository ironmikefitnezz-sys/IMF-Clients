Readiness Assessment
You came in today with excellent sleep, solid energy, and zero soreness, which means your body is fully ready to work and we are going to take full advantage of that.
Warm-Up
5 to 7 minutes to get your back, shoulders, and arms ready to move well. Band pull-aparts or arm circles: 2 sets of 15 slow, controlled reps to open up your shoulders and upper back before loading them. Cat-cow stretch on all fours: 10 slow reps moving your spine up and down to wake up your back and loosen your mid-section. Tricep overhead stretch: Hold each arm for 20 seconds to lengthen the back of your arms before pressing and pushing. Light lat pulldown or straight-arm cable pulldown: 1 set of 15 reps at very light weight just to get blood moving into your back before the main work begins.
Main Block
Today is Back and Triceps. Your chest and biceps worked yesterday so we are keeping the focus entirely on pulling movements for your back and pushing movements for the back of your arms. All supersets today pair non-competing muscles so one group rests while the other works. Superset 1 — complete all 4 sets before moving on Exercise A: Seated Cable Row -- 4 sets of 10 reps. Sit tall, pull the handle into your lower chest, squeeze your shoulder blades together at the end of each rep, then let your arms extend fully before the next pull. Exercise B: Tricep Rope Pushdown -- 4 sets of 12 reps. Stand at the cable machine, keep your elbows tucked at your sides, push the rope down and slightly apart at the bottom, fully straightening your arms each rep. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 2 -- complete all 4 sets before moving on Exercise A: Lat Pulldown (wide grip) -- 4 sets of 10 reps. Sit with your thighs secured under the pad, lean back slightly, and pull the bar down to your upper chest while driving your elbows toward your hips. Control the bar back up slowly. Exercise B: Overhead Tricep Extension with Rope (cable) -- 4 sets of 12 reps. Face away from the cable machine with the rope attachment set high, bring both hands behind your head, then press forward and up until your arms are fully extended. Control the return. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 3 -- complete all 3 sets before moving on Exercise A: Single-Arm Dumbbell Row -- 3 sets of 10 reps each side. Place one knee and hand on a bench for support, keep your back flat and parallel to the floor, and row the dumbbell up toward your hip. Lower it with control. Exercise B: Dumbbell Tricep Kickback -- 3 sets of 12 reps each side. Hinge forward slightly at the hips with a flat back, pin your upper arm parallel to the floor, then extend your forearm back until your arm is straight. Squeeze at the top. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Finisher -- traditional set, no superset Straight-Arm Cable Pulldown -- 3 sets of 15 reps. Stand facing the cable machine with a straight bar set high, keep a slight bend in your elbows, and pull the bar down in an arc to your thighs using only your back. This isolates the outer back and is a great way to finish the session with a pump.
Cool-Down
5 to 7 minutes to bring your body down and protect your recovery. Child's pose with arms extended: Hold for 30 to 45 seconds to stretch your back and lats after all the pulling work. Doorway or cable machine chest and shoulder stretch: Hold each side for 20 to 30 seconds. This is a gentle counter-stretch after the pulling motion. Overhead tricep stretch: Hold each arm for 20 to 30 seconds to lengthen the back of your arms after all the pushing. Slow deep breathing: 5 slow inhales through your nose and exhales through your mouth to bring your heart rate down and signal recovery to your body.
Coach Note
You are planning to use two cable stations in the same superset during Supersets 1 and 2, so scope out your setup before you start and be ready to swap to a dumbbell alternative if the gym is busy. Today was a clean, high-output session and sessions like this done consistently are exactly what closes the gap between where you are now and the definition you are working toward.
