Readiness Assessment
You came in with a perfect check-in today, Justin, sleep was great, energy is high, and you are carrying zero soreness, so you are set up for one of your best sessions of the week.
Warm-Up
Light Bike
Easy pedaling with low resistance.
5 minutes at an easy pace to raise your heart rate and get blood moving to the shoulders and upper body.
Arm Circles
Arms out to the sides, draw small then bigger circles.
30 seconds forward then 30 seconds backward to open up the shoulder joints before loading.
Wall Slides
Back against a wall, slide your arms up and down like making a snow angel.
10 slow reps to wake up the muscles around the shoulder blades and set your posture for pressing and raising movements.
Standing Hip Circles
Stand on one leg, draw big slow circles with the other knee.
10 circles each direction to loosen the hips after yesterday's leg session before you stand and move.
Main Block
Circuitformat today. Move through all exercises before resting. Take 15 to 20 seconds to transition between exercises and rest 90 seconds after completing the full circuit. Complete 4 rounds total.
Circuitcomplete all exercises before resting
1Dumbbell Shoulder Press, Seated
Sit with back support, press dumbbells overhead.
12 reps. Press with control on the way up and lower slowly on the way down.
2Cable Lateral Raise
Lift a cable handle out to your side to shoulder height.
Note: Image shows a seated version. Stand tall and lift one handle out to your side.
12 reps each side. Keep a soft bend in the elbow and raise to shoulder height.
3Plate Front Raise
Hold a weight plate and lift it in front to shoulder height.
12 reps. Lift to shoulder height and lower with control, keeping the movement smooth throughout.
4Face Pull
Pull a rope to your face with elbows high, squeezing the upper back.
15 reps. Pull to forehead height and hold briefly at the end range of each rep.
5Cuban Press
Pull weights up like an upright row, rotate them forward, then press overhead.
12 reps. Move through the full rotation deliberately and do not rush the press at the top.
6Band Pull-Apart
Hold a band in front and pull it apart.
15 reps. Keep a slight bend in the elbows and pull the band apart in a smooth controlled arc.
Rest: 90 seconds, then repeat
Complete 4 rounds total
Cool-Down
Cross-Body Shoulder Stretch
Pull one arm across your chest with the other arm.
30 seconds each side to release tension built up through the lateral and pressing work.
Upper Trap Stretch
Tilt your ear toward your shoulder and gently add pressure with your hand.
30 seconds each side to ease any tightness that built up through the raises and presses.
Overhead Triceps Stretch
Bend one arm behind your head and gently press the elbow with the other hand.
30 seconds each side to open up the back of the arm after the overhead work.
Lying Knee-to-Chest Stretch
On your back, hug one knee toward your chest.
60 seconds to decompress the lower back and SI joint after standing and seated loading.
Diaphragmatic Breathing, 90/90 Position
On your back, calves on a bench, take slow deep belly breaths.
2 minutes of slow steady breathing to bring your heart rate down and close out the session.
Coach Note
