Readiness Assessment
You came in today firing on all cylinders, Justin, great sleep, high energy, and zero soreness is exactly the kind of day we build real progress on.
Warm-Up
5 to 7 minutes to get your shoulders and upper body moving and ready to load. Arm circles, forward and backward, 20 seconds each direction. Keep them big and controlled. Band pull-aparts or light cable face pulls, 2 sets of 15 reps. Focus on squeezing your shoulder blades together at the end of each rep. Shoulder pass-throughs with a resistance band or broomstick, 10 slow reps. This opens up the shoulder joint and gets the rotator cuff warmed up before loading. Light lateral raises with very light dumbbells, 1 set of 15 reps. Just waking up the muscles, no effort yet.
Main Block
Superset 1 - complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Dumbbell Shoulder Press - 4 sets of 10 reps. Press the dumbbells straight overhead from shoulder height, lower them slowly back down. Exercise B: Cable Rope Face Pull - 4 sets of 12 reps. Set the cable at upper chest height, pull the rope toward your face with elbows flaring out, squeeze at the end. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 2 - complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Dumbbell Lateral Raise - 3 sets of 12 reps. Raise both arms out to the sides until they reach shoulder height, lower slowly. Exercise B: Cable Upright Row - 3 sets of 12 reps. Pull the bar or rope straight up along your body to chin height, elbows lead the movement. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 3 - complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Dumbbell Front Raise - 3 sets of 10 reps. Raise one arm at a time or both together straight in front of you to shoulder height, lower slowly. Exercise B: Machine Rear Delt Fly (reverse pec deck) - 3 sets of 12 reps. Sit facing the pad, grab the handles and pull your arms back wide squeezing the back of your shoulders together. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Finisher Cable Lateral Raise - 3 sets of 15 reps each side. Stand sideways to the cable, pull from low to shoulder height with a slight bend in the elbow. Slow and controlled each rep.
Cool-Down
5 to 7 minutes to bring your heart rate down and keep your shoulders feeling good. Cross-body shoulder stretch, hold 30 seconds each side. Pull one arm across your chest and hold gently. Overhead tricep stretch, hold 30 seconds each side. Reach one arm overhead and let the elbow bend behind your head. Doorway or cable machine chest opener stretch, hold 30 to 45 seconds. Step through a doorway with arms on the frame and gently lean forward to open the front of the shoulder. Neck side tilts, slow and gentle, 5 reps each side. Let gravity do the work, no pulling.
Coach Note
Today is exactly the kind of session that starts moving the needle toward that muscle definition goal, full effort, smart shoulder work, and you are holding two stations for the supersets so have a plan for which cable and dumbbell spots you will rotate between before you start.
