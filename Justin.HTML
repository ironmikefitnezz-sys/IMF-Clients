Readiness Assessment
You came in today firing on all cylinders, Justin, and the numbers back it up, so we are going to put that energy to good use on leg day.
Warm-Up
5 to 7 minutes to get the hips, knees, and lower back ready to work. Banded side steps: Place a light resistance band just above your knees and step side to side for 2 sets of 15 steps each direction. This wakes up the outer hips and glutes before loading them. Bodyweight glute bridge: Lie on your back, feet flat on the floor, and push your hips straight up toward the ceiling. Hold one second at the top and lower slowly. Do 2 sets of 12 reps. This activates the glutes and helps your SI joint feel settled before the session. Hip circle stretches: Stand and draw slow large circles with your hips, 10 rotations each direction. Loosens up the hip joint and improves range of motion for the work ahead. Ankle circles and calf raises: 10 ankle circles per foot followed by 15 slow bodyweight calf raises. Gets the lower leg and ankle ready to support loading.
Main Block
Superset 1 -- complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Goblet squat -- Hold a dumbbell vertically at your chest, feet shoulder width apart, and squat down keeping your chest tall and your knees tracking over your toes. 4 sets of 12 reps. Exercise B: Lying leg curl machine -- Lie face down on the leg curl machine and curl both legs up toward your glutes in a controlled motion. Lower slowly. 4 sets of 12 reps. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 2 -- complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Leg press machine -- Sit in the leg press, place feet shoulder width apart at mid-height on the platform, and press the weight away from you without locking your knees out fully at the top. Lower slowly until your knees reach about a 90 degree bend. 4 sets of 10 reps. Exercise B: Hip abduction machine (outer thigh machine) -- Sit in the machine and push your knees outward against the pads as far as comfortable, then slowly bring them back together. 4 sets of 15 reps. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 3 -- complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Leg extension machine -- Sit in the machine with your back flat against the pad and extend both legs out until straight, hold one second at the top, and lower slowly. 3 sets of 12 reps. Exercise B: Hip adduction machine (inner thigh machine) -- Sit in the machine and squeeze your knees inward against the pads, hold one second, and release slowly. 3 sets of 15 reps. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Finisher -- traditional set Glute bridge with dumbbell: Lie on your back with a dumbbell resting on your hips. Push your hips up to the ceiling, squeeze hard at the top for two seconds, and lower with control. 3 sets of 15 reps, rest 60 seconds between sets. This directly supports your SI joint rehab by building the glute strength that takes pressure off the lower back.
Cool-Down
5 to 7 minutes to bring everything down and keep the hips and lower back feeling good. Figure four stretch: Lie on your back and cross one ankle over the opposite knee. Gently pull the uncrossed leg toward your chest until you feel a stretch in the crossed hip. Hold 30 seconds each side. A priority stretch for your right hip flexibility. Knee to chest stretch: Lie flat and pull one knee at a time into your chest and hold for 20 to 30 seconds each side. Decompresses the lower back after loading. Seated piriformis stretch: Sit upright in a chair and cross one ankle over the opposite knee. Sit tall and gently press down on the raised knee until you feel a stretch deep in the hip. Hold 30 seconds each side. Slow diaphragmatic breathing: Lie flat on your back, hands on your stomach, and breathe in slowly through your nose for 4 counts, then out through your mouth for 6 counts. Repeat for 5 breaths. Brings your nervous system back down after a strong effort.
Coach Note
