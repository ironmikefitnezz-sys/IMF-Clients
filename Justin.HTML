Readiness Assessment
Justin, you came in tonight with a nine across the board on sleep and energy, zero soreness, and nearly no stress — that is about as dialed in as it gets, so today we are going all in.
Warm-Up
5 minutes to get your body ready to move. Start with 2 minutes of light cardio — a brisk walk on the treadmill or easy bike to get blood moving. Then move into hip circles — stand with your hands on your hips and make slow, controlled big circles with each hip, 10 circles each direction. This warms up the hip joint and starts to loosen the area around your SI joint. Next, do 10 glute bridges on the floor — lie on your back, feet flat, push your hips up toward the ceiling and hold for 2 seconds at the top, then lower. This activates your glutes before loading them. Finish with 10 bodyweight box squats at a slow controlled pace — sit back onto a bench or box, pause, and stand. This grooves the movement pattern before you add weight.
Main Block
Today is Legs. All metrics are at their best, so we are running full superset pairs. You will need to set up two stations for each pairing — plan ahead or have a dumbbell alternative ready if the gym is busy. Superset 1 — complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Goblet Squat — hold a dumbbell or kettlebell at your chest, feet shoulder-width apart, sit back and down keeping your chest up, then drive through your heels to stand. Do 4 sets of 12 reps. Exercise B: Leg Curl Machine (lying or seated) — set the weight, curl your heels toward your body against the resistance, squeeze at the top, and lower slowly. Do 4 sets of 12 reps. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 2 — complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Leg Press Machine — feet shoulder-width apart at mid-height on the platform, press the weight away without locking your knees out at the top, lower slowly until your knees are at about 90 degrees. Do 4 sets of 10 reps. Exercise B: Hip Abduction Machine (the machine where you push your knees outward against pads) — sit tall, push your knees out against the resistance to the comfortable end range, then bring them back slowly. Do 4 sets of 15 reps. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 3 — complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Leg Extension Machine — sit with your back fully against the pad, extend your legs out straight against the resistance, squeeze at the top for one second, then lower slowly. Do 3 sets of 15 reps. Exercise B: Glute Bridge with Dumbbell (loaded) — lie on your back, place a dumbbell on your hips and hold it steady, feet flat on the floor, drive your hips up and squeeze your glutes hard at the top for 2 seconds, then lower. Do 3 sets of 15 reps. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat.
Cool-Down
5 to 7 minutes to bring your body back down. Seated Figure-Four Stretch — sit on a bench or the floor, cross one ankle over the opposite knee, gently press the raised knee down and hinge slightly forward until you feel a stretch deep in the outer hip. Hold 30 seconds each side. This directly addresses your right hip flexibility, which is a rehab priority. Lying Knee-to-Chest Pull — lie on your back, pull one knee gently into your chest, hold 20 to 30 seconds, switch sides. Keeps the lower back and SI joint area relaxed after loading. Seated Hamstring Stretch — sit on the edge of a bench, extend one leg straight out in front of you with your heel on the floor, sit tall and hinge gently forward from your hips until you feel a pull behind your knee. Hold 30 seconds each side. Deep Belly Breathing — finish with 5 slow deep breaths, breathing in through your nose for 4 counts and out through your mouth for 6 counts. Lowers your nervous system back to baseline.
