Readiness Assessment
Justin, you came in today firing on all cylinders -- great sleep, high energy, and zero soreness means we have every green light to build something solid for your shoulders.
Warm-Up
5 to 7 minutes to get your shoulders and upper body ready to move well. Arm circles -- stand tall and make slow, controlled circles with both arms, forward for 30 seconds then backward for 30 seconds. This wakes up the shoulder joint and gets blood moving. Band pull-aparts or towel pull-aparts -- hold a resistance band or rolled towel in front of you at chest height with both hands, then pull it apart by squeezing your shoulder blades together. Do 15 slow reps. This activates the muscles around the back of your shoulder and helps stabilize the joint before loading it. Shoulder rolls -- shrug your shoulders up toward your ears, roll them back, and let them drop. Do 10 slow rolls forward and 10 backward. Light lateral raises with no weight or very light dumbbells -- 15 reps just to feel the movement pattern before adding load.
Main Block
Today is shoulder day and your readiness is excellent, so we are running full superset pairs. You will need two stations for each superset, so scout your setup before starting each pair. Superset 1 -- complete all 4 sets before moving on Exercise A: Seated Dumbbell Shoulder Press -- sit on an upright bench, hold a dumbbell in each hand at shoulder height with palms facing forward, press both dumbbells straight up until your arms are nearly straight, then lower them back to shoulder height with control. Do 4 sets of 10 reps. Exercise B: Cable Face Pull -- attach a rope handle to a cable machine set at upper chest height, grab one end of the rope in each hand, step back until there is tension on the cable, pull the rope toward your face by driving your elbows out and back, then return slowly. Do 4 sets of 12 reps. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 2 -- complete all 3 sets before moving on Exercise A: Dumbbell Lateral Raise -- stand with a dumbbell in each hand at your sides, keep a slight bend in your elbows, and lift both arms out to the sides until they reach shoulder height, then lower slowly. Do 3 sets of 12 reps. Exercise B: Dumbbell Front Raise -- stand with a dumbbell in each hand resting on the front of your thighs, palms facing down, lift one arm straight in front of you to shoulder height then lower it as you raise the other arm in an alternating rhythm. Do 3 sets of 10 reps per arm. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 3 -- complete all 3 sets before moving on Exercise A: Machine Rear Delt Fly -- sit facing the pad of the rear delt fly machine, grab the handles with both hands, and pull them back and out to the sides in a wide arc, squeezing the back of your shoulders at the end of the movement, then return slowly. Do 3 sets of 12 reps. Exercise B: Dumbbell Arnold Press -- sit on an upright bench, hold a dumbbell in each hand in front of your shoulders with palms facing you, as you press the dumbbells up rotate your palms to face forward at the top, then reverse the rotation as you lower them back down. Do 3 sets of 10 reps. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat.
Cool-Down
5 to 7 minutes to bring your heart rate down and keep your shoulders feeling good. Cross-body shoulder stretch -- bring one arm across your chest and hold it there with your opposite hand, feeling a stretch in the back of your shoulder. Hold 30 seconds each side. Overhead tricep and shoulder stretch -- reach one arm straight up, bend your elbow so your hand drops behind your head, and use your other hand to gently press the elbow further back. Hold 30 seconds each side. Chest opener stretch -- interlace your fingers behind your back, straighten your arms, and gently lift them while opening your chest toward the ceiling. Hold 30 seconds. Slow deep breathing -- finish with 5 slow breaths in through your nose and out through your mouth to bring your nervous system down from the session.
Coach Note
This is a strong baseline session for your shoulders, Justin -- the combination of pressing, raising, and rear delt work hits every angle and sets a clear benchmark to build from as you work toward that definition goal.
