Readiness Assessment
You came in today firing on all cylinders, Justin, great sleep, high energy, and zero soreness means you are ready to push and make real progress toward that definition goal.
Warm-Up
5 to 7 minutes to get your shoulders, chest, and arms ready to work. Arm circles forward and backward, 10 slow rotations each direction, to loosen up your shoulder joints before loading them. Band pull-aparts or light resistance band rows, 15 reps, to activate the muscles between your shoulder blades and prep your upper back. Shoulder rolls forward and backward, 10 each direction, just to get blood moving through the joint. Light dumbbell chest press, 1 set of 15 reps with very light weight, to prime the chest and front shoulder before your working sets. Alternating dumbbell curls, 1 set of 15 reps with light weight, to wake up the biceps before loading them.
Main Block
Superset 1 - complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Dumbbell Bench Press - 4 sets of 10 reps. Lie flat on a bench, lower the dumbbells to chest level with control, then press back up. Keep your feet flat on the floor. Exercise B: Dumbbell Hammer Curl - 4 sets of 10 reps. Stand with dumbbells at your sides, palms facing each other, curl both up together without swinging your elbows forward. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 2 - complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Incline Dumbbell Press - 3 sets of 10 reps. Set the bench to a moderate incline, press the dumbbells up from chest height and lower with control. This targets the upper chest. Exercise B: Cable Curl (low cable with straight bar or EZ bar attachment) - 3 sets of 10 reps. Stand facing the cable machine, keep your elbows tucked at your sides, and curl the bar up to shoulder height. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 3 - complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Cable Chest Fly (cable crossover or pec deck machine) - 3 sets of 12 reps. Bring both handles together in front of your chest with a slight bend in your elbows. Focus on squeezing your chest at the midpoint. Exercise B: Concentration Curl - 3 sets of 12 reps. Sit on a bench, rest your elbow on the inside of your thigh, curl one dumbbell at a time with full control. Great for isolating and shaping the bicep peak. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 4 - complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Push-Up - 3 sets of 12 reps. Standard push-up with hands just outside shoulder width. Lower your chest to just above the floor and press back up. If needed, elevate your hands on a bench to reduce difficulty. Exercise B: Incline Dumbbell Curl (straight-arm curl on an incline bench) - 3 sets of 10 reps. Sit on an incline bench holding dumbbells with your arms hanging straight down, then curl up. This stretch position builds the lower portion of the bicep. Rest: 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat.
Cool-Down
5 to 7 minutes to bring your body down and reduce next-day soreness. Cross-body shoulder stretch, 30 seconds each arm. Pull one arm across your chest and hold. You should feel a stretch in the back of your shoulder. Chest doorway stretch or hands-clasped-behind-back stretch, 30 to 45 seconds. Open up the front of your chest after all that pressing. Overhead tricep stretch, 20 seconds each arm. Reach one arm overhead, bend at the elbow, and use your other hand to gently pull the elbow back. This releases the tricep and shoulder. Bicep wall stretch, 20 seconds each arm. Place your palm flat on a wall with your arm straight, then slowly turn your body away until you feel a stretch in the front of your arm and chest. Slow deep breathing, 5 breaths in through the nose for 4 counts, out through the mouth for 6. This brings your heart rate and nervous system back down before you leave the gym.
