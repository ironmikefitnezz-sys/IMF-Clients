Readiness Assessment
You came in today with excellent sleep, high energy, and almost no stress, so your body is fully ready to work and this is exactly the kind of day where you make real progress toward that muscle definition goal.
Warm-Up
5 to 7 minutes to get the chest, shoulders, and biceps ready to move well. Arm circles, both forward and backward, 30 seconds each direction. This loosens up the shoulder joints before any pressing or curling begins. Band pull-aparts or light dumbbell front raises, 2 sets of 15 reps with a light weight. This wakes up the muscles across the front and back of your shoulders. Light dumbbell curls, 1 set of 15 reps with a very easy weight. This gets blood moving into the biceps before your working sets. Push-up plank hold, 20 to 30 seconds. This activates your core and prepares your chest and shoulders for the pressing pattern without loading your spine.
Main Block
Today is Tuesday, which means Chest and Biceps. Your readiness is excellent across the board, so you are getting the full superset format. Mild leg soreness is noted but that muscle group is not being trained today, so it has no impact on today's session. You will need two stations for each superset, so plan ahead to claim your spot or have a dumbbell backup ready. Superset 1 of 4, complete all sets before moving on. Exercise A: Dumbbell Bench Press, flat bench pressing both dumbbells up from chest level. 4 sets of 10 reps. Exercise B: Dumbbell Hammer Curl, standing curl with palms facing each other the whole time. 4 sets of 12 reps. Rest 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 2 of 4, complete all sets before moving on. Exercise A: Incline Dumbbell Press, flat bench set to a 30 to 45 degree angle, pressing up and slightly inward at the top. 4 sets of 10 reps. Exercise B: Concentration Curl, seated with one elbow braced against your inner thigh, curl one arm at a time. 4 sets of 10 reps each arm. Rest 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 3 of 4, complete all sets before moving on. Exercise A: Cable Chest Fly, set both cable pulleys high and bring the handles together in front of your chest in a wide hugging motion, keeping a slight bend in your elbows. 3 sets of 12 reps. Exercise B: EZ Bar Curl or Straight Barbell Curl, standing curl with both hands on the bar, controlled on the way down. 3 sets of 12 reps. Rest 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat. Superset 4 of 4, complete all sets before moving on. Exercise A: Machine Chest Press or Push-Up, chest press machine set to a mid-chest height, or bodyweight push-ups if machine is unavailable. 3 sets of 12 reps. Exercise B: Cable Curl, using the low pulley cable with a straight bar or rope attachment, curl up slowly and lower slowly. 3 sets of 12 reps. Rest 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat.
Cool-Down
5 to 7 minutes to bring everything back down and support recovery. Doorway chest stretch or reaching both arms behind you and gently squeezing your shoulder blades together, hold 30 seconds, repeat twice. This releases tightness from all the pressing work. Bicep wall stretch, place your palm flat against a wall at shoulder height with your arm straight and gently turn your body away until you feel a stretch along the front of your arm. Hold 30 seconds each side. Shoulder cross-body stretch, pull one arm across your chest and hold it with your other arm just above the elbow. Hold 30 seconds each side. Deep belly breathing, 5 slow breaths in through the nose and out through the mouth. This shifts your nervous system out of the working state and supports recovery.
Coach Note
Today's session is designed to hit both chest and biceps with enough volume and variety to build the muscle definition you are after, and days like this one where your energy and sleep are both dialed in are the sessions that actually move the needle, so make the most of it by staying focused on control and full range of motion on every rep rather than just chasing heavier weight.
