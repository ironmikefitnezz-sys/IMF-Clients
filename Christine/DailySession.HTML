Readiness Assessment
Christine, your numbers look solid today and a headache does not change that — we will keep the session as planned but make sure you stay hydrated throughout.
Warm-Up
5 minutes total. Start with 2 minutes of slow marching in place to get blood moving. Then do 10 slow hip circles each direction standing with hands on hips. Finish with 10 body weight squats moving at a comfortable pace, focusing on sitting back into your heels. This gets your hips and knees ready to work.
Main Block
Today is Tuesday: Upper Body Superset 1 — complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Seated Cable Row — 3 sets of 12 reps. Sit at the cable row machine, grip the handle, pull it into your lower chest keeping your back straight, then slowly return. Rest 0 seconds before moving to B. Exercise B: Tricep Pushdown (cable or rope) — 3 sets of 12 reps. Stand at the cable machine, grip the rope or bar at chest height, push it straight down until your arms are fully extended, then slowly return. Rest 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat from A. Superset 2 — complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Dumbbell Chest Press on Flat Bench — 3 sets of 10 reps. Lie on a flat bench holding a dumbbell in each hand at chest level, press them up until your arms are extended, then lower slowly. Rest 0 seconds before moving to B. Exercise B: Dumbbell Bicep Curl — 3 sets of 10 reps. Stand or sit holding a dumbbell in each hand at your sides, curl both up toward your shoulders with palms facing up, then lower slowly. Rest 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat from A. Superset 3 — complete all sets before moving on Exercise A: Dumbbell Lateral Raise — 3 sets of 12 reps. Stand holding light dumbbells at your sides, raise both arms out to the side until they reach shoulder height, then lower slowly. Rest 0 seconds before moving to B. Exercise B: Seated Leg Curl Machine — 3 sets of 12 reps. Sit in the leg curl machine, position the pad on top of your lower legs, curl them downward as far as comfortable, then return slowly. Rest 45 to 60 seconds after completing both exercises, then repeat from A.
Cool-Down
5 minutes total. Hold a chest stretch in a doorway or with your hands clasped behind your back for 30 seconds. Then do a seated or standing bicep stretch by extending one arm straight out and gently pulling your fingers back for 20 seconds each side. Finish with a slow overhead tricep stretch holding each arm for 20 seconds. Take slow deep breaths throughout.
Coach Note
