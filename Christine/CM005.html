Readiness Assessment
You came in today fully recharged with great sleep, high energy, and nothing holding you back, so we are going to make the most of it.
Warm-Up
Light Treadmill Walk
Easy walk to raise your heart rate and warm up.
5 minutes, gets the blood moving and prepares your legs for today's work.
Standing Hip Circles
Stand on one leg, draw big slow circles with the other knee.
10 reps each direction, loosens up the hips before loading them.
Leg Swings
Hold a wall, swing one leg forward and back like a pendulum, then turn and swing it side to side across your body.
10 reps each leg, front to back and side to side, wakes up the legs and hips.
Bodyweight Squat to Box
Sit back until you lightly touch a box or bench, then stand up.
10 reps, rehearses the squatting pattern you will use in today's session.
Main Block
Leg Press
Sit in the machine, push the platform away with feet in the middle.
3 sets of 12 reps, rest 60 seconds between sets.
Goblet Squat
Hold one weight at your chest, squat down and stand up.
3 sets of 12 reps, rest 60 seconds between sets.
Hip Thrust, Machine
Back on a pad, drive your hips up against the machine's resistance.
3 sets of 12 reps, rest 60 seconds between sets.
Seated Leg Curl
Sit in the machine and pull the pad down by bending your knees.
3 sets of 12 reps, rest 60 seconds between sets.
Leg Extension
Sit in the machine and straighten your knees against the pad.
3 sets of 12 reps, rest 60 seconds between sets.
Hip Abduction Machine
Sit and push your knees apart against pads.
3 sets of 15 reps, rest 45 seconds between sets.
Cool-Down
Figure-Four Stretch
Lie on your back, ankle on opposite knee, pull the legs toward you to stretch your glute.
45 seconds each side, releases tension in the glutes and outer hips after today's work.
Supine Hamstring Stretch with Strap
Lie on your back, loop a strap around one foot, raise the straight leg.
45 seconds each leg, lengthens the back of the legs after all the pressing and curling.
Lying Knee-to-Chest Stretch
On your back, hug one knee toward your chest.
45 seconds each side, eases any tightness through the lower back and hips.
Standing Quad Stretch
Stand and pull one heel toward your glute.
30 seconds each leg, takes care of the front of the legs after the leg press and extensions.
Diaphragmatic Breathing, 90/90 Position
On your back, calves on a bench, take slow deep belly breaths.
2 minutes, brings your heart rate down and helps your body shift into recovery mode.
Coach Note
This is a solid starting point, Christine, and every rep you put in today is building the foundation that will carry your energy and health forward from here.
